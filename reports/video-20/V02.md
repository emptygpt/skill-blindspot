# Blindspot report: shorts (AgriciDaniel/claude-shorts)

Map: video-short v0.2.1 (extends video v0.2.1)    Profile: none    Date: 2026-11-17

Skill under audit: AgriciDaniel/claude-shorts, SKILL.md, with references/scoring-rubric.md, caption-styles.md, platform-specs.md, remotion-patterns.md (read); scripts/ and remotion/ code not read (untrusted). Local copy has scripts/, so behaviour claims below come from SKILL.md and references only.

## Scope
- Cuts vertical short clips out of a long video the user supplies: preflight, transcribe, score candidates, user approves, snap cuts to audio boundaries, extract, reframe, render animated captions in Remotion, export per platform, validate.
- Responsibility ends at exported and validated MP4 files in ./shorts/. No upload, no titles, no scheduling.
- Does not write scripts, shoot, record, or add music. Input is existing footage (talking head, screen, podcast).

## Step 2 list (written before the map was opened)
1. Check that the user owns or may reuse the source video (guests, third-party clips): searched rights, owner, permission; not found.
2. Check that a cut clip does not misrepresent what the speaker meant: only "Standalone coherence" scoring.
3. Transcript accuracy, proper nouns and caption text matching the spoken words: partly (filler removal, "fix obvious errors").
4. Clip title, caption text and hashtags for posting: not found (only an on-screen hook line).
5. Cover or thumbnail frame: not found.
6. UI safe zone for captions: present in references/caption-styles.md.
7. Audio: loudness and noise: loudnorm appears only in references/platform-specs.md.
8. Reframe correctness (face tracking, several speakers, screen content): content type detection; no visual check.
9. Caption language or translation, burned-in versus sidecar: not found.
10. Retention editing of the clip (jump cuts, zooms, b-roll): not found.
11. A human look at the finished captioned render: only a technical validation script.
12. Ending or loop treatment: not found.
13. Feedback from published results: not found.
14. Batch naming and scheduling of many clips: not found.
15. Platform duration caps and encoding: present (Step 10, platform-specs).

## Findings

### Needed
1. **No check that the user may reuse the source video** [model+map]
   Map node: assets.rights-acquisition, assets.copyright-fair-use, comp.originality-reposts, comp.declare-original-repost, short.original-content, short.no-foreign-watermark — source: O*NET 31.0 Database, task statements for video-related occupations, "27-2012.00 task: Obtain rights to scripts or to such items as existing"
   Why: Searched for rights, owner, permission, guest, watermark. The pipeline accepts "INPUT_FILE" and the approval step covers segments only. A clipper is the typical way to repost others' content, and the platform rules the map cites treat reposts and foreign watermarks differently from original work.
2. **Captions are edited text laid on unchanged timestamps and never compared with the audio** [model+map+trace]
   Map node: captions.accuracy-accessibility, captions.edit, asm.av-sync — source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M14 caption accuracy for accessibility"
   Why: Step 4 removes fillers and "Consolidate[s] incomplete sentence fragments" while "Keep all timestamps unchanged"; the render uses transcript_cleaned.json. Removed or merged words leave captions out of step with speech, and validate.sh checks only the file, not the captions.

### Worth considering
1. **Approved cut points are changed after approval and the result is not shown** [trace]
   Map node: asm.review-revision — source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M10 review-and-revision workflow"
   Why: Step 7 snaps start and end ("From this point forward, use snapped_segments.json") after the user approved; only deltas are reported. No look at the final rendered clip by a person; Step 10 validation is technical.
2. **Reframe quality is not verified** [model+map]
   Map node: short.quality-floor, edit.review-footage — source: 抖音社区自律公约, "视频画质模糊，无完整内容"
   Why: Content type and crop are computed ("Face-tracked center crop") and reported as text. Nothing views a frame to check a face is in frame or a speaker change was tracked.
3. **A clip can change the speaker's meaning** [model]
   Map node: none (unsourced)
   Why: Scoring rewards hook strength and emotion; "Standalone coherence" is about understandability, not about fairness to what the speaker meant. Unsourced.
4. **No post title, description, hashtags or cover frame** [model+map]
   Map node: pack.title, pack.tags, pack.thumbnail, short.cover, short.thumbnail-framing, pack.title-type — source: YouTube Help: Thumbnail & title tips, "How to write titles"
   Why: Only the on-screen hook text is produced.
5. **Loudness and audio cleanup are not in the main skill** [model+map]
   Map node: sound.mix, sound.audio-editing, sound.ai-cleanup — source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M10 an audio mix"
   Why: loudnorm appears only in references/platform-specs.md; no instruction to measure or fix noisy source audio.
