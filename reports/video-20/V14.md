# Blindspot report: knowledge-explainer-skill (V14)

Map: video-long 0.2.1 (extends video), combined with base map; Profile: none    Date: 2026-11-17

Audit target: znyupup/knowledge-explainer-skill, SKILL.md (about 5 KB), its template list and examples/script.md format. cli.js, parser.js and generator.js were not run. The SKILL.md and README mention no audio, voice or subtitle feature.

## Scope
- Turns a markdown or notes document into an animated explainer video: the agent writes a script.md of template sections with durations, writes custom JSX for complex visuals, and renders with Remotion locally (default 1280x720, 30 fps).
- Starts at a user document; ends at an MP4 on disk that the user is told to watch. No publishing, no filming.
- The skill says nothing about narration, audio or captions, so the output is understood to be a silent video.

## Findings

### Needed
1. **Nothing checks that the explanation is true before rendering** [model+map+trace]
   Map node: comp.fact-check, script.ai-assisted, script.revision — source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M14 verifying AI-drafted facts"
   Why: The agent writes script.md from the document and invents custom physics and algorithm animations, then renders. The only check is the duration and the render log. A wrong formula, number or simulation would ship; the source document is never compared against the final frames.
2. **"Explainer video" with no narration, audio or captions** [model+map]
   Map node: script.narration, sound.narration-recording, sound.mix, sound.music-selection, captions.create, captions.accuracy-accessibility — source: ESCO classification v1.2.1, skills of video-related occupations, "write voice-overs"
   Why: Searched voice, audio, TTS, subtitle, caption: no match in the skill. Everything is on-screen text and animation, so a "知识科普视频" is silent, has no caption/transcript file, and has no accessibility rule beyond a 14 px label size.
3. **Rendered output is not inspected frame by frame** [model+map+trace]
   Map node: asm.review-revision, edit.rough-cut — source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M10 review-and-revision workflow"
   Why: Step 5 reads only duration and the log, then asks the user to watch. Agent-written custom JSX is rendered in full without a preview or still at each scene, so overflow, overlap or blank scenes are found only by the user.
4. **No audience, goal or hook decision** [model+map]
   Map node: topic.focus, topic.project-definition, script.story-message, script.hook — source: YouTube for Creators: Longform, "Find your focus"
   Why: The workflow goes from "读取用户文档" to template choice. No question about who the video is for, what the viewer should be able to do, or how the first seconds earn attention (the Title template is a card).

### Worth considering
5. **No rights check and no disclosure for AI-made content** [model+map]
   Map node: assets.rights-acquisition, assets.copyright-fair-use, assets.font-licensing, comp.ai-disclosure, comp.media-law-ethics — source: O*NET 31.0 Database, task statements for video-related occupations, "27-2012.00 task: Obtain rights to scripts or to such items as existing video footage."
   Why: The user's document, emoji, fonts and any examples are used without a rights note; no AI label guidance.
6. **Packaging and platform format are left out** [model+map]
   Map node: pack.title, pack.thumbnail, pack.description-chapters, pack.tags, asm.platform-standards, long.chapters — source: YouTube Help: Thumbnail & title tips, "How to write titles"
   Why: Output is a bare MP4 at 1280x720; no title, cover, description, or platform spec (aspect ratio, length).

### Minor
7. **Remaining absent or shallow nodes, not individually argued** [map]
   Nodes: script.outline, script.shot-list-storyboard, script.audience-retention, assets.file-management, assets.generative-material, assets.trademarks, edit.principles, sound.effects, captions.transcription, captions.edit, captions.translate, captions.reach, captions.silent-viewing, pack.title-type, pub.export, long.open-close-key-moments, long.end-screen, long.end-screen-timing
