# Blindspot report: tmakersas/revid-skills (revid-research-make-and-post)

Map: video-short 0.2.1 (extends video 0.2.1)    Profile: none    Date: 2026-11-17

## Scope
- Entry skill `revid-research-make-and-post`: searches viral short videos on a topic, writes a 20-45 s script from the strongest hook pattern, prices it, renders through Revid's cloud API, waits for the file, and posts or schedules it on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram or X.
- Starts at a topic from the user; ends at a published or scheduled post and its link. Sub-skills read: `revid-api-foundations`, `revid-prompt-to-video`, `revid-script-to-video`, shared poll-status doc and credit docs.
- All visuals, voice and edit are produced by the remote service (stock or generated media); nothing is filmed or edited by hand.
- Needs a Revid account, API key and connected social accounts; analytics after the post link is outside the stated flow.

## Findings

### Needed
1. **Video can be posted publicly without anyone having watched it** [trace+map]
   Map node: asm.review-revision - source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M10 review-and-revision workflow"; short.quality-floor - source: 抖音社区自律公约, "视频画质模糊，无完整内容"; pub.precheck - source: 抖音规则中心 > 社区细则「社区规则」(规则文件目录), "发布前自查：快速检测使用指南"
   Why: Step 5 says "Share the link and let the user watch it", but step 6 says to confirm account, caption and time "unless they gave all three already" and never makes approval of the finished video a gate; the Rules section also lacks one. A failed or off-topic render is posted as is. [model also expects a QA step]
2. **No fact-check of script claims and no policy or AI-label check before a public post** [model+map]
   Map node: comp.fact-check - source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M14 verifying AI-drafted facts"; comp.ai-disclosure - source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M14 Use AI responsibly"; comp.community-guidelines - source: YouTube for Creators: Helpful Resources, "Community Guidelines" (also: comp.submission-rules, comp.violation-categories, comp.copyright-strike, comp.media-law-ethics, acct.platform-rules, asm.platform-standards)
   Why: Searched "fact", "verify", "source", "label", "disclos", "policy", "guideline": none in the entry skill or foundations. The script is written from viral examples and posted with synthetic voice and generated visuals; the only pre-post rule is "always confirm the account, the caption and the time".
3. **Music, stock and generated-media rights and limits are never addressed** [model+map]
   Map node: assets.music-licensing - source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M1 music-licensing and copyright basics"; short.music-eligibility - source: YouTube Help: Get started creating YouTube Shorts, "Music eligibility for YouTube Shorts"; assets.rights-acquisition - source: O*NET 31.0 Database, task statements for video-related occupations, "27-2012.00 task: Obtain rights to scripts or to such items as existing video footage." (also: assets.generative-material, assets.copyright-fair-use, comp.originality-reposts, short.original-content)
   Why: `"music": {"generateMusic": true}`, `media.type: stock-video` and models up to veo3/sora2 are used with no mention of licence terms or platform music rules; "Never copy a creator's words" is the only originality rule though the video imitates a winning pattern.
4. **Failure path conflicts with the "never render twice" rule; cost is stated but not approved** [trace+map]
   Map node: ana.tracking - source: YouTube for Creators: Understand your audience, "How to track your data in YouTube Studio"; script.revision - source: ESCO classification v1.2.1, skills of video-related occupations, "edit scripts"
   Why: The Rules say "Never call render_video twice for the same video: poll the first pid instead", while foundations lists `status == "failed"` and `insufficient_credits`; the entry skill gives no recovery for a failed pid. Step 3 tells the user the cost but does not wait for an OK before render, so credits can be spent unconfirmed. Poll interval also differs (20-30 s here, 5-8 s in foundations).
