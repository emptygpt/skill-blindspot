# Blindspot report: codex-video-pipeline (zoglmk/codex-video-pipeline)

Map: video-short v0.2.1 (extends video v0.2.1)    Profile: none    Date: 2026-11-17

Skill under audit: zoglmk/codex-video-pipeline, SKILL.md with references/quality-gates.md, research-and-script.md, project-contract.md, captions.md, cover-system.md (read); first-run, cross-platform, example-run skimmed; scripts/ not read (untrusted). Typed "short" in the candidate list, but the defaults are 16:9 1920x1080 videos of 80 s or more; vertical is only an option. Short-form nodes are judged against that.

## Scope
- Full pipeline for Chinese AI-knowledge self-media videos: topic and evidence, script and motion plan, assets, narration, aligned captions, HyperFrames render, quality gates, covers, publish package.
- Responsibility ends at a verified 产出/ folder (MP4, two covers, publish copy); it does not upload.
- Default 16:9 1920x1080 at 30 fps; "短视频通常 80 秒以上". No on-camera filming; assets come from imagegen, user files or Pexels.

11. Contrast and flashing checks: contrast present; flashing not found.
12. Post-publish analytics: not found.

14. Opening hook: present.
15. Cleanup and archive: present with confirmation.

## Findings

### Needed
1. **No label or disclosure step for AI-generated images and synthetic voice** [model+map]
   Map node: comp.ai-disclosure, assets.generative-material — source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M14 Use AI responsibly"
   Why: Searched for 标识, 声明, AI-generated, disclosure. The skill generates covers, backgrounds and narration with AI and builds a "publish-ready package" whose 发布文案 content is unspecified.
2. **"Done" can be declared with nobody having watched the final video** [model+trace]
   Map node: asm.review-revision, pub.precheck — source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M10 review-and-revision workflow"
   Why: "所有硬门通过后才能宣布完成" and project.py verify. The checklist (quality-gates.md) is self-assessed, and "Verify" checks files, receipts and encodings. The viewing items (hook, PPT feel, music over voice) have no named reviewer and no evidence file.

### Worth considering
1. **Default format is horizontal 16:9 though typed as a short** [map]
   Map node: short.vertical-frame, short.safe-zone, short.duration-limit, short.claims-over-one-minute — source: YouTube Help: Understand three-minute YouTube Shorts, "Eligibility for three-minute Shorts"
   Why: 1920x1080 and 80 s or more by default; no UI safe zone, no vertical layout rules for the video itself.
2. **Audio mix has no measurement** [model+map]
   Map node: sound.mix, sound.effects, sound.music-selection — source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M10 an audio mix"
   Why: Only "BGM 不盖住旁白"; no loudness target or check, no SFX step.
3. **Title, tags, discoverability and silent viewing** [model+map]
   Map node: pack.title, pack.title-type, pack.tags, pub.discoverability, captions.silent-viewing — source: YouTube Help: Thumbnail & title tips, "How to write titles"
   Why: The publish copy file is named but its contents are not specified.
4. **Platform rules, community guidelines and copyright strikes are not covered** [map]
   Map node: comp.community-guidelines, comp.copyright-strike, comp.submission-rules, comp.medical-claims — source: YouTube for Creators: Helpful Resources, "Community Guidelines"
   Why: Only authoritative sources for high-risk topics.
5. **No step to revise the script, audience data or topic cadence** [model+map]
   Map node: script.revision, topic.audience-research, topic.cadence, topic.new-formats, short.trending-inspiration — source: ESCO classification v1.2.1, skills of video-related occupations, "edit scripts"
   Why: Script is written once; topic scoring uses no platform data.

   Map node: comp.sponsor-disclosure, pre.releases, assets.trademarks — source: Disclosures 101 for Social Media Influencers (November 2019), "How to Disclose"
