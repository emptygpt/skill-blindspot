# Blindspot report: SoloMCN video pipeline (ymybxx/SoloMCN, make-video and the skills it calls)

Map: video-short v0.2.1 (extends video v0.2.1)    Profile: none    Date: 2026-11-17

Skill under audit: ymybxx/SoloMCN, defaults/skills/make-video/SKILL.md plus write-script, research-topic, curate-topics, daily-topics, account-ideas, teardown-video, precheck-content, explainer-video (SKILL, video.md, references/review.md, bgm.md), revise-video, voice-over, review-data, docs/skills.md (read); the workbench server, scripts/ and stage-montage not read in depth. Audited as one pipeline of 15 skills. Chinese-language, unattended: the skills run in the background of a workbench app with no one to answer questions.

## Scope
- Pipeline for short Chinese videos: topic radar and daily topics, research with sources, storyboard script, optional pre-publication check, video generation (HyperFrames motion graphics, TTS voice, synthesised BGM and SFX), revisions from human notes, review of published data.
- Responsibility ends at an MP4 saved to the workbench card (save_video); publishing is done by the workbench app, not by these skills. review-data reads published numbers afterwards.
- Runs unattended: "没有人能回答问题". No filming of people; a presenter may be an illustrated character.

## Step 2 list (written before the map was opened)
1. Labelling of AI-made or synthetic content as platforms require: searched AI label, disclosure, 标识; found the opposite (removed on purpose).
2. Fact-checking of script claims against sources: present (research report with sources).
3. Rights to reused screenshots, third-party clips, music: partly ("出处记在后台", "不用来路不明的音乐").
4. Title, description, hashtag and cover copy: partly (3 titles, cover text).
5. Platform spec and duration limits: partly.
6. Loudness and audio QA: present (BGM -14 LUFS).
7. Caption accuracy against audio: present (transcribe back-check).
8. A human gate before the final render: none; unattended by design; pre-check is advisory.
9. Opening hook in the first seconds: present.
10. Retention pacing: present.
11. Safe zone for platform UI: not found.
12. Defamation and fairness when naming real products or people ("不设限制").
13. Analytics and feedback loop: present (review-data).
14. Photosensitivity: partly ("不用快速闪烁").
15. Duplicate or near-duplicate content across accounts: partly (no repeat topics).

## Findings

### Needed
1. **AI and fictional-content labels are removed on purpose, and the staged-content rule is reversed** [model+map+trace]
   Map node: comp.ai-disclosure, comp.staged-content — source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M14 Use AI responsibly"
   Why: make-video, write-script, revise-video, precheck-content and explainer-video all say the same: no AI label, fictional or schematic note, or disclaimer is added before the final stage, and precheck "不能导致不通过" if they are missing. The map cites platform rules that require labels on AI-made or staged content. Only a "用户在成片阶段处理" hand-off exists, and no later step in these skills checks it was done.
2. **Third-party screenshots, clips and recordings are used as the main picture with "no limits" and no rights step** [model+map]
   Map node: assets.rights-acquisition, assets.copyright-fair-use, comp.screen-recording-original, comp.copyright-strike, short.no-foreign-watermark — source: O*NET 31.0 Database, task statements for video-related occupations, "27-2012.00 task: Obtain rights to scripts or to such items as existing"
   Why: "除此之外不设限制：可以点名...用他们公开的网页、帖子截图和官方视频片段" and video.md "真实素材当主画面...满幅". Sources go to notes only. No check that a clip is licensed, watermark-free, or that the added work makes a recording original.
3. **No human gate before the final render, and the pre-check is advisory** [model+trace]
   Map node: pub.precheck, asm.review-revision, comp.community-guidelines — source: 抖音规则中心 > 社区细则「社区规则」(规则文件目录), "发布前自查：快速检测使用指南"
   Why: make-video: "不提任何问题，不等任何回复"; explainer-video: "点「生成视频」就是渲染的确认"; precheck "只给参考意见". The sub-agent review covers visual defects only and "最多改一轮". Nothing blocks a video that breaks a platform rule.

### Worth considering
1. **Engagement-bait prompt is required in every script** [map]
   Map node: comp.engagement-bait — source: 抖音规则中心 规则文件「诱导互动」, "骗取互动"
   Why: "【结尾互动】一句引导评论的话" is mandatory; no rule separates a plain prompt from baiting.
2. **No publishing copy beyond three titles and cover text; no tags** [model+map]
   Map node: pack.tags, pack.title-type, pub.discoverability, short.cover, pack.thumbnail — source: 哔哩哔哩《UP主创作手册 通用篇》(2020), "3 标签"
   Why: Only 【标题备选】 and 【封面文案】; topics or tags are not chosen.
3. **No UI safe zone or platform duration cap** [model+map]
   Map node: short.safe-zone, short.duration-limit, asm.platform-standards, pub.export — source: TikTok Business Help Center: Creative best practices for performance a, "keeping your content visible within the UI safe zone"
   Why: Searched for 安全区 and platform limits; only "【平台差异】" text.
4. **Naming real products and people has no fairness check** [model+map]
   Map node: comp.media-law-ethics, assets.trademarks — source: Video: Digital Communication & Production, 5th edition (2023), "Ch3 Media Law / Media Ethics"
   Why: The one red line is forgery; naming rivals or people is explicitly allowed.
5. **Feedback loop stops at summary advice** [model+map]
   Map node: ana.ab-test, ana.early-ctr, ana.refresh-old, ana.reach-restriction — source: YouTube for Creators: Optimize your content, "A/B test thumbnails and titles"
   Why: review-data gives a text review; no experiment or reach-limit diagnosis.
