# Blindspot report: revid-research-make-and-post

Map: video-short 0.2.1 (extends video 0.2.1)    Profile: none    Date: 2026-11-17

Also read: sibling skills revid-api-foundations, revid-prompt-to-video, revid-script-to-video, revid/SKILL.md, shared/ and docs/ (grep covered the repository root, code included).

## Scope
- Takes a topic, researches viral videos (search_viral_videos), drafts a 20 to 45 s script, renders it through the Revid MCP server (voice, captions, generated music, stock or AI visuals, 9:16), and posts or schedules it on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram or X.
- Starts at the user's topic or request. Ends at get_publish_status and giving the user the post link.
- Does not film, edit by hand, set up accounts, run ads or monetisation, or measure results after the post.
- Revid does the cutting, so shooting, pre-production and sound recording nodes are out of scope.

## Findings

### Needed
1. **No disclosure or label for AI-made content before a public post** [model+map]
   Map node: comp.ai-disclosure — source: Video Production Course Online syllabus, "M14 Use AI responsibly"; 抖音规则中心 "抖音内容标识使用规范" and "抖音关于AI生成内容的答疑公告". Also assets.generative-material, short.ai-tools, comp.staged-content.
   Why: grep terms disclos, AI-generated, label, synthetic, AIGC, staged over the skill folder and repository root found only a product-ad description line and no instruction. Step 4 sets AI voice, generated music and stock or AI visuals, and Step 6 posts publicly. The Step 6 and Rules confirmation covers account, caption and time only. In scope because the skill does the posting.
   Fix: in Step 6 and Rules, add a check of the platform's AI-content label for each target platform, and have the user confirm it with the account, caption and time.
2. **Script claims are never fact-checked** [model+map]
   Map node: comp.fact-check — source: Video Production Course Online syllabus, "M14 verifying AI-drafted facts"; O*NET 27-3041.00 "Verify facts, dates, and statistics". Also topic.subject-research, script.ai-assisted.
   Why: grep terms fact, verify, accurate, claim, source over the skill folder and repository root found nothing that applies. Step 1 researches viral patterns, not the subject. Step 2 has the model draft the script and only asks for the user's OK. The skill is a one-shot loop that ends in a public post.
   Fix: in Step 2, add a pass that lists each factual claim, number or date in the script with its source, and drops or flags those that cannot be sourced, before the user's OK.

### Worth considering
3. **No platform policy or standards check before posting** [model+map]
   Map nodes: pub.precheck, asm.platform-standards, comp.community-guidelines, comp.submission-rules, comp.violation-categories, comp.media-law-ethics, comp.minors, comp.medical-claims, comp.dangerous-content, comp.offsite-leads — source: 抖音规则中心 community rules; YouTube Shorts policies as listed in the map.
   Why: grep guideline, policy, moderation, nsfw, link found only an nsfwFilter default in shared/default-payload.json, which filters the render, not the post. Only partly covered, so not Needed. Step 6 caption rules do not mention links or contact details.
   Fix: add a short pre-post checklist to Step 6 (policy topics such as health claims and minors, no off-platform links in the caption).
4. **The render is not reviewed and failure is not handled in this skill** [model+trace+map]
   Map nodes: asm.review-revision, captions.edit, captions.accuracy-accessibility, short.quality-floor.
   Why: Step 5 polls "until it returns videoUrl" and says "let the user watch it". There is no branch for status failed, no timeout, no checklist for caption errors or mispronounced names, and no way back to Step 2 or 4 except the "never render twice" rule. The foundations skill has a failed branch and a 10 minute timeout, but this skill links to it only for install. Poll cadence here (20 to 30 s) also differs from the other skills (5 to 8 s).
   Fix: in Step 5, state the failed and timeout outcomes and link the polling section; add a review of captions and pacing, and say whether a change means a new render or an edit.
5. **Cover, title and search wording are not chosen** [model+map]
   Map nodes: pack.thumbnail, pack.title, pack.title-type, short.cover, pub.discoverability.
   Why: grep cover, thumbnail, title found hasToGenerateCover only in other skills' payloads. The Step 4 payload leaves the cover unset, and Step 6 writes a caption and hashtags only. YouTube needs a title; the skill never asks for one.
   Fix: add cover and title to Step 4 and Step 6 for platforms that use them.
6. **Music rights and copyright for generated or reused material** [model+map]
   Map nodes: assets.music-licensing, short.music-eligibility, assets.copyright-fair-use, comp.copyright-strike, comp.declare-original-repost, sound.music-selection.
   Why: grep copyright, licen, royalt, music found only the generateMusic flag. The "Never copy a creator's words" rule covers words, not visuals or sound taken from the researched videos.
   Fix: in Step 4, note that the generated track is cleared for the target platforms, or say what to check, and extend the no-copying rule to footage and audio.
7. **No branch for a failed or rejected post, and no duplicate-post guard** [model+trace+map]
   Map nodes: comp.returned-submission, comp.no-duplicate-upload.
   Why: Step 7 gives the post link "once it is live" with no failed or rejected state; Step 6 handles only tokenInvalid. The one idempotency rule is about render_video. grep duplicate, twice, retry showed the repo's TODO.md lists idempotency as open.
   Fix: in Step 7 and Rules, say what to report when get_publish_status shows a failure, and that a video is not posted twice to the same account.
8. **Nothing reads results after posting** [model+map]
   Map nodes: ana.insights, ana.tracking, ana.early-ctr, short.engaged-views, short.analytics-app.
   Why: grep analytics, views, performance, insights found none for this skill. Step 1 uses viral data going in, but no step brings the post's results back. Scope ends at the post link, so this is a judgement call.
   Fix: add an optional final step that checks the post's early numbers and records the pattern used.
9. **Sponsorship disclosure** [model+map]
   Map node: comp.sponsor-disclosure.
   Why: grep sponsor, paid, brand, #ad found no instruction on this. Only matters when the video promotes a product or brand.
   Fix: in Step 6 add a question whether the post is paid or gifted and how to mark it.
