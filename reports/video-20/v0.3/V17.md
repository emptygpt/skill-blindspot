# Blindspot report: video-toolkit (entry skill video_toolkit)

Map: video 0.2.1    Profile: none    Date: 2026-11-17

Counts: 118 nodes: 35 covered, 14 shallow, 21 absent, 48 not-applicable, 0 excluded

## Scope
- Makes short explainer videos (9:16 or 16:9) from a brief: the agent writes a scene spec, a detached job voices, illustrates, captions, builds and QCs it, and the agent delivers the MP4. Also offers session routes (Remotion, Blender, moviepy templates) and single-asset tools.
- Responsibility ends at a QC-checked MP4, with optional private YouTube upload (`youtube_upload.py`) and subtitle and chapter files (`publish_kit.py`).
- Does not involve filming people, live broadcast, account setup, monetisation or community management.
- The folder holds only SKILL.md; the repo around it (.claude/commands, .claude/skills, docs, tools) was searched before rating findings. Nine files named in SKILL.md are not in the skill folder, but all except `design.md` (a per-project file) and `.attribution.txt` (a runtime output) exist in the repo.

## Step 2 list (written before the map)

2. Permission for real people's photos and cloned voices (absent in the job route).
3. Licence and copyright of inputs and generated assets (shallow).
4. Platform policy check: kids, health, sponsorship (absent).
5. AI-content disclosure on upload (not in this file).
6. Title, description, tags, thumbnail guidance (thin).
7. Human approval before publishing (covered: private default, show person the review points).
8. Reading results after publishing (not in this file).
9. Translations or second language (absent).

## Findings

### Needed
1. **No research or fact-check of what the narration says** [model+map]
   Map node: comp.fact-check, topic.subject-research, script.ai-assisted. Sources: O*NET 27-3041.00 "Verify facts, dates, and statistics, using standard reference sources" <https://www.onetcenter.org/database.html>; Video Production Course syllabus, "M14 verifying AI-drafted facts" <https://www.graduateschool.edu/outlines/video-production-course-online-self-paced-syllabus.pdf>
   Why: the skill's main job is "A short explainer on a topic ... from a brief", written by the model ("you are the LLM", line 125) or an expander. `copy_lint` checks dashes and stock phrasing only. Grep on skill folder and repo root (code included) for fact, factual, research, cite, accura, hallucinat, verify claim: hits only in parody-short (says to stop fact-checking jokes), the howto `facts.json` (howto route only), and a note that LTX garbles text. Scope check: writing the narration is inside the skill, and a wrong figure ships baked into voice and captions.
   Fix: add to step 1 of the quick path (Write the spec) a line to check each figure, date and claim in the narration against a source before `submit`, and to tell the person in the delivery message what was checked.

### Worth considering
2. **No permission step for people's photos, clips and cloned voices** [model+map]
   Map node: pre.releases, assets.rights-acquisition. Sources: Video Production handbook (2016 revision), "Talent Release" <https://www.a2gov.org/media/01pgn5jo/produce.pdf>; O*NET 27-3041.00 "Arrange for copyright permissions."
   Why: `kit` and `file` scenes use "their photos" (line 160) and `voice` accepts "a cloned voice" (line 173). Grep terms consent, likeness, permission, release, real person, impersonat: the parody-short skill ("Real people stay internal") and docs/blender.md cover only their own routes; `.claude/commands/voice-clone.md` has no consent line. Partly covered, so not Needed.
   Fix: in the `kit` and `voice` bullets, say to use only the kit owner's own photos and voice, or ones the owner has permission for.
3. **No policy, licence and rights check before delivery** [model+map]
   Map node: assets.music-licensing, assets.copyright-fair-use, assets.generative-material, assets.trademarks, comp.community-guidelines, comp.copyright-strike, comp.advertiser-friendly, comp.media-law-ethics, comp.submission-rules, comp.violation-categories, comp.minors, comp.medical-claims, comp.dangerous-content, comp.sponsor-disclosure. Sources: YouTube for Creators, Helpful Resources, "Community Guidelines" and "Made for Kids" <https://www.youtube.com/creators/resources/>; FTC Disclosures 101 <https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers>
   Why: grep terms sponsor, affiliate, medical, minor, kids, trademark, copyright, licen[sc]e: only music-and-sound.md (sfx licence), docs/qwen-edit-patterns.md and modal-setup.md (non-commercial model licences) and `selfDeclaredMadeForKids: False` as an upload default. The SKILL.md line 421 licence note covers sfx only. Platform and kit videos for a business could carry health claims or a sponsor.
   Fix: add a short pre-delivery list to "Collect the result" or the YouTube upload paragraph: commercial-use licence of the model and music used, sponsor or health claims, kids audience.
4. **Entry file does not route to publishing metadata, thumbnail and AI disclosure** [model+map]
   Map node: comp.ai-disclosure, comp.staged-content, pack.thumbnail, pack.title, pack.title-type, pack.tags, pack.playlists, pub.discoverability, pub.precheck, pub.scheduled-publish. Sources: YouTube Help "Thumbnail & title tips" <https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12340300>
   Why: grep terms synthetic, disclos, thumbnail, title, publishAt: covered in `.claude/commands/publish.md` (`--synthetic-media`, title advice, metadata review), `.claude/commands/thumbnail.md` and docs/youtube-upload.md. SKILL.md line 448 shows `youtube_upload.py` with no `--synthetic-media` and never names /publish or /thumbnail, so an agent reading only this file misses the disclosure.
   Fix: in the YouTube paragraph (line 448), point to `.claude/commands/publish.md` and `.claude/commands/thumbnail.md` and mention the synthetic-media flag.
5. **No step for reading results after publishing** [model+map]
   Map node: ana.insights, ana.tracking. Source: YouTube for Creators, "How to track your data in YouTube Studio" <https://www.youtube.com/creators/grow/understand-your-audience/>
   Why: grep terms youtube_stats, analytics, retention: `tools/youtube_stats.py` and `.claude/skills/youtube-stats/` exist; SKILL.md never mentions them. After-publish work is partly outside scope, hence not Needed.
   Fix: add one line after the upload paragraph naming `youtube_stats.py`.
