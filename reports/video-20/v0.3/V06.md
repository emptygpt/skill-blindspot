# Blindspot report: youtube-shorts-factory

Map: video-short 0.2.1 (extends video 0.2.1)    Profile: none    Date: 2026-11-17

## Scope
- Takes a user-supplied YouTube Shorts URL, analyses it (7-axis forensic), adapts it by 30%+, writes a storyboard and Story JSON, generates images, builds an HTML storyboard, then drives an external pipeline (rfn-video-pipeline, `run.py`) plus Google Veo/TTS/MusicFX to produce `final_subtitled.mp4`.
- Responsibility ends at the subtitled mp4 in `output/{slug}/`. It does not upload, title/tag, or publish.
- No filming of people, no recording of sound, no account or analytics work. Korean-language family-drama style only.
- The pipeline scripts (`run.py`, `add_subtitles.py`) and the `rfn-video-pipeline` skill are not in the repository (repo root = skill folder; only SKILL.md, README.md, three resources files), so what they check could not be determined.

## Findings

### Needed
1. **No originality or copyright check on a workflow built to clone another creator's video** [model+map]
   Map node: comp.originality-reposts, short.original-content, assets.copyright-fair-use, assets.rights-acquisition, comp.copyright-strike, short.remix, short.no-bulk-posting — source: YouTube for Creators: Shorts, "Non-original Shorts" / "Ineligible Shorts views"; YouTube for Creators: Helpful Resources, "Copyright", "Fair use"
   Why: grep on skill folder and repo root (code included) for `저작권|copyright|licen|fair|original|원작|출처|strike` finds only the MIT licence and adaptation-guide.md:7 "저작권 침해 없이 ... 최소 30% 이상 변경이 필요", an unsourced assertion that a 30% change rate avoids infringement. The change rate is self-scored in the Step 3 checklist (✅/❌) and never checked against anything. Step 3 keeps the emotional structure, Open Loop count, payoff timing and pacing of the original, i.e. the parts a reference creator would call the work. Repo root is the skill folder, so no script covers it. In scope: Steps 1-3 are the core of the skill.
   Fix: in Step 3 replace the "30% = safe" claim with a stated originality test (what must differ, what may not be copied, such as specific scene sequence and payoff) and a note that the platform's original-content and copyright rules, not a percentage, decide; add the source to adaptation-guide.md.

2. **No labelling of AI-generated, photoreal "Korean actor" footage or of staged scenes** [model+map]
   Map node: comp.ai-disclosure, comp.staged-content, assets.generative-material — source: Video Production Course Online syllabus, "M14 Use AI responsibly"; 抖音规则中心 "虚假摆拍"
   Why: grep on folder and repo root for `disclos|합성|altered|AI 생성|label|허구|가상|staged|재연` finds nothing. Step 5 forces "realistic Korean facial features ... hyperrealistic"; the sample story is an elderly father and a memo, easily taken as a real event. Step 5 sets a style lock but no limit on what generated footage may depict or how it is declared. Delivery stops at the mp4, but the file is made to be uploaded and the labelling decision belongs with the creation step, since the pipeline output carries no flag.
   Fix: add to Step 4 (Story JSON) or Step 7 a short rule: mark the video as AI-generated and fictional in a caption/on-screen line or in a hand-off note listing the label the creator must set at upload; include the real-person likeness limit for generated faces.

3. **The generated video is never reviewed; only still frames are** [model+map+trace]
   Map node: asm.review-revision, asm.av-sync, edit.review-footage, short.ai-tools — source: O*NET 27-2012.00 task "Review film, recordings, or rehearsals to ensure conformance to production and broadcast standards"; ESCO "conduct content quality assurance"
   Why: grep on folder and repo root for `최종|final|review|검증|재생|시청|QC|sync` finds frame checks only (SKILL.md:205-211 이미지 검증) and one comment "# 7. 사용자 최종 확인" before Phase 2 (workflow-steps.md:130). Step 7 runs `--resume` for the clips, then `add_subtitles.py`, and ends with the output tree; nothing says to play clips or the final file, check lip/voice/music sync, drifting faces between clips, or garbled burned-in text, and nothing loops back to regenerate one clip (the loop exists only for frames, Step 5). Veo/minimax clips cost up to $1.20 each (SKILL.md:156), so a bad clip is both a quality and cost risk.
   Fix: add a Step 8 (or end of Step 7) that has the agent open each clip and final_subtitled.mp4, list defects per frame, and re-run only the bad clip, with the same keep-and-regenerate pattern used in Step 5.

### Worth considering
4. **Audio is named but not designed: narration script, music choice, mix** [model+map]
   Map node: script.narration, sound.narration-recording, sound.music-selection, sound.effects, sound.mix, assets.music-licensing, short.music-eligibility — source: Video Production Course Online syllabus, "M1 music-licensing and copyright basics"; YouTube for Creators: Shorts, "Choose your music"; YouTube Help "Music eligibility for YouTube Shorts"
   Why: grep for `나레이션|TTS|music|BGM|라이선스|licen` finds only the Step 7 table rows (SKILL.md:269-270). Story JSON has a `subtitle` field but no narration text field, and the Phase 3 assembly (`final.mp4`) does not say whether voice and music are mixed in. Whether the pipeline does so could not be determined.
   Fix: in Step 4 add a narration line to the scene fields and in Step 7 say who produces voice and BGM, at what level, and that AI music terms are checked for the target platform.

5. **Shorts format rules are absent: length, safe zone, text pace, opening seconds** [model+map]
   Map node: short.duration-limit, short.safe-zone, short.text-pace, short.hook-first-seconds, asm.platform-standards, captions.accuracy-accessibility, captions.edit, edit.principles — source: YouTube Help "Understand three-minute YouTube Shorts"; TikTok Business Help Center creative best practices (safe zone, first 6 seconds, first 3 seconds)
   Why: grep for `3분|180|길이|safe|세이프|duration|UI` shows only `target_duration_seconds: 60` as an example (SKILL.md:132). The Step 2 hook axis asks about "첫 1~3초" for the reference only (SKILL.md:56); the new video has no first-seconds rule. Subtitle position and reading speed are not set, and the CSS overlay for AI text (SKILL.md:244-258) fixes only the storyboard HTML, not the video.
   Fix: in Step 4 add constraints for the target (max length, subtitle inside a safe area, short lines, the offer stated within the first seconds) and have the Step 8 review check them.

6. **No check against platform content policy before hand-off** [model+map]
   Map node: comp.community-guidelines, comp.violation-categories, comp.submission-rules, comp.advertiser-friendly, comp.media-law-ethics — source: YouTube for Creators: Helpful Resources, "Community Guidelines"
   Why: grep for `policy|guideline|규정|가이드라인|monetiz|수익` finds nothing. Content is mostly benign family drama, hence not Needed.
   Fix: add a one-line pre-hand-off check in Step 7 pointing to the target platform's guidelines.
