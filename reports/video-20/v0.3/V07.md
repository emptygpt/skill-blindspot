# Blindspot report: viralforge

Map: video-short 0.2.1 (extends video 0.2.1)    Profile: none    Date: 2026-11-17

## Scope
- Analysis and planning tool: deconstructs a reference short video into a DNA report, replicates its structure into a word-anchored VFML project, and produces 10 costed variants for ad A/B testing.
- Responsibility ends at the DNA report, the project skeleton and the variant list with costs; generation models are cloud-side and the demo renders are pre-made.
- It does not film, edit by hand, upload, publish or run a channel, and it forbids face swaps and copying footage, music or script.

## Findings

### Needed
1. **Claims in the replaced product script are never researched, checked or disclosed** [model+map]
   Map node: topic.subject-research, comp.fact-check, comp.medical-claims, comp.sponsor-disclosure, comp.honest-review. Sources: see `blindspot.py sources --map video-short comp.fact-check comp.medical-claims comp.sponsor-disclosure comp.honest-review topic.subject-research`.
   Why: Step 4 tells the agent to replace "product, selling points, script" and the purpose is ad A/B variants (SKILL.md:3, 14, 37), yet no step gathers facts about the new product or checks the new selling points. The shipped demo shows the risk: a skincare ad with a specific ingredient claim (README.md:111, 124). Grep terms on skill folder and repo root (code included): `fact|claim|verify` (only "Never claim a render" and a PR checkbox on demo claims), `sponsor|advert` (no instruction), `medical|health|cosmetic` (no match), `consent|release` (no match for disclosure). Scope check: the skill writes the new script and variants, so this is inside scope; publishing is not.
   Fix: In Workflow step 4 (Replicate), add a sub-step that lists every factual or health/efficacy claim in the new script with its source, drops the unsourced ones, and flags sponsored or ad content for disclosure; carry the list into the report in Output style.

2. **The hard boundaries are never checked before delivery** [trace+map]
   Map node: asm.review-revision, script.revision, short.ai-tools. 
   Why: Step 4 and Hard boundaries say the replica must not copy the original script, footage or music, and step 6 says every output carries AI labels, but no step compares the new script or shots with the reference or confirms the label exists on the output. Grep terms: `similar|overlap|plagiar|originality|duplicate` (no hits in cli, scripts, mcp or src), `label` (CLI, MCP and analyze.py only emit shot labels, nothing checks the AI label). The only "pre-delivery" line is "Never claim a render or model call that did not happen" (SKILL.md:82). Scope check: the verifying is inside the skill since it builds the replica.
   Fix: Add a final step after Variant (before the report) that diffs script wording and shot structure against the reference, checks that `aiDisclosure` is true and the on-frame label is present, and records the result with the cost ledger.

### Worth considering
3. **Music and voice for the replica are forbidden to copy but no replacement is specified** [model+map]
   Map node: assets.music-licensing, sound.music-selection, assets.generative-material
   Why: SKILL.md:86 only says "not footage, music, or the original script". Grep terms: `music|bgm|licens` (docs say demo uses locally synthesized BGM, CHANGELOG.md:25; the skill never says what to use).
   Fix: In step 4, say what music and voice sources are allowed (own, licensed, synthesized) and what the generated shots may depict.

4. **Avatar permission and content-safety checks on generated people and scenes** [model+map]
   Map node: pre.releases, comp.staged-content, comp.minors, comp.dangerous-content, comp.community-guidelines, comp.violation-categories, comp.advertiser-friendly
   Why: "Only use the user's own or licensed avatars" (SKILL.md:40) gives no way to confirm a licence, and nothing mentions minors, dangerous acts, staged-as-real scenes or platform content rules for the variants. Grep terms: `consent|likeness|permission` (only the reference-file permission at SKILL.md:24), `minor|child|danger` (no match in skill).
   Fix: Add to step 6 (Label) a short permission check for avatars and a pass over the variants against the target platform's content rules.

5. **No loop from variants to results** [model+map]
   Map node: ana.ab-test, topic.audience-research, script.audience-retention
   Why: Ten variants are made "for A/B testing" (README.md:31, PLAN.md:80) but the skill says nothing on how to choose audiences, run the test, or feed results back; retention is reported only for the reference (SKILL.md:32). Grep terms: `A/B|ab-test|metric` (marketing text only).
   Fix: In step 5, say how audiences are chosen and what result to record per variant for the next round.

6. **Short-form format limits for the replica** [map]
   Map node: short.duration-limit, short.safe-zone, short.text-pace, asm.platform-standards, captions.create, captions.edit, script.narration, script.story-message
   Why: VFML sets ratio 9:16 and duration, but nothing covers the length limit, UI safe zone, caption pace, caption creation for the new script or narration writing. Grep terms: `safe.zone|safezone` (no match), `caption|subtitle` (only reading the reference caption).
   Fix: Add a format check to step 4 against the target platform's length and safe-zone rules, and a caption step for the replaced script.

### Minor
7. **Hook promises and engagement tricks copied from the reference** [map]
   Map node: short.honest-loop-payoff, comp.engagement-bait
   Why: Step 3 and 4 transfer a hook formula such as "反常识否定" (SKILL.md:77) without telling the agent to check that the new video pays off the promise or avoids bait tactics. Grep terms: `payoff|bait|loop` (no match).
   Fix: Add one line to step 4 on checking payoff and bait.
