# Blindspot report: make-video (with explainer-video, stage-montage, voice-over, transcribe)

Map: video-short 0.2.1 (extends video 0.2.1, 142 nodes)    Profile: none    Date: 2026-11-17

## Scope
- Pipeline audited as one unit: `make-video` (entry, registration, report) calls `explainer-video` (HyperFrames text-and-image video, with presenter, BGM, review and check scripts) or `stage-montage` (cut-together real stage footage), plus `voice-over` and `transcribe`. `revise-video` and the script, research and precheck skills are siblings that make-video does not call.
- Input is an approved script on a content card; output is a rendered vertical or horizontal MP4 plus a contact sheet, registered through `save_video` with background notes. It runs unattended and cannot ask questions.
- Responsibility ends at the registered MP4. Titles, cover images, platform copy and uploading are done by the app's `server/publish` module; script writing and precheck are other skills.
- It does not film people. AI labels, disclaimers and "staged" notices are deliberately left to the user at the final stage (make-video "作品正文与后台记录"); this is a stated design decision, so those nodes are not-applicable.
- Not determinable: `/faceless-explainer`, `/hyperframes` and `/media-use` are external skills installed by `scripts/setup.mjs`, not in the repository. Anything they might cover (for example safe zones or mixing defaults) could not be checked.

## Findings

### Needed
1. **The finished file is never checked against what the script and platform require** [model+map+trace]
   Map node: asm.platform-standards, sound.mix, short.duration-limit — source: O*NET 27-2012.00 task "Review film, recordings... to ensure conformance to production and broadcast standards"; Video Production Course syllabus "M10 an audio mix"; YouTube Help "Understand three-minute YouTube Shorts"
   Why: make-video step 3 only says "确认成片文件存在，用 `ffprobe` 或渲染输出读出时长". `film-check.mjs` measures only fast motion, empty frames and blue-purple share (video only). `bgm.md` says "BGM 不写 `volume`，用默认的人声下铺底音量", and the voice level is never measured. The duration limit is checked only in stage-montage ("总长不超过系列时长"); explainer-video has no post-render duration check. Grep terms: ffprobe, 分辨率, 静音, 响度/LUFS, 混音, 音量, 最长, 画幅, audio stream, run on the skill folder and the repository root. Hits are limited to the bgm loudness script, the presenter/publish helpers and the stage-montage line above; none checks the final render. Scope check: rendering is inside the skill, and `server/publish` only reads orientation and duration at upload time, after the file is already registered as done.
   Fix: add a final-file check to make-video step 3 (or `references/review.md` "渲染后：体检"): probe aspect and resolution against the script, duration against script and series length, an audio stream that is not silent, and a rough voice-versus-music loudness; record the result in `notes`.

### Worth considering
1. **Rights and originality of third-party footage and song audio** [model+map]
   Map nodes: assets.rights-acquisition, assets.copyright-fair-use, assets.music-licensing, comp.copyright-strike, comp.originality-reposts, comp.declare-original-repost, comp.screen-recording-original, short.original-content, short.music-eligibility, short.claims-over-one-minute — sources: O*NET 27-2012.00 "Obtain rights to... existing video footage"; YouTube for Creators Shorts "Non-original Shorts"; YouTube Help three-minute Shorts "Third-party claimed content for Short that is over one minute"; bilibili 投稿规范 "4、投稿类型" and "5、视频出处"
   Why: `stage-montage` builds the whole video from other people's live recordings and a commercial song track, and video.md step 11 puts the source "写进 notes，不加到画面上". `make-video` 内容要求 says "除此之外不设限制". Grep terms: 版权, copyright, 侵权, 原创, 搬运, 授权, licen, run on the skill folder and the repository root. The only hits are research-topic "版权风险", `README.md` 217 "版权……请自行判断" and unrelated server code. This looks like a deliberate stance, so it is not Needed, but nothing tells the agent to put a repost flag or claim risk into `notes` for the user.
   Fix: in make-video step 3 `notes`, and stage-montage/video.md step 11, add a line listing which clips and which song are third-party, so the user can set the repost declaration and clear music rights when publishing.
2. **Text placement and reading pace for vertical feeds** [model+map]
   Map nodes: short.safe-zone, short.text-pace — source: TikTok Business Help Center "Creative best practices" (UI safe zone; 5-10 words per second)
   Why: video.md 画面质量 sets only size ("至少 28 像素高") and "屏上大字一般 10 个字以内". Grep terms: 安全区, safe zone, 遮挡, 字幕位置, 底部, run on both roots; only `film-check.mjs` "底部 15% 当字幕带" matches, and that is for ignoring the caption band. `/faceless-explainer` might define caption position, but it is not available here.
   Fix: add one rule to video.md 「画面质量」 about keeping captions and key text out of the bottom and right-edge app-button zones, and a reading-speed cap for subtitles.
3. **Rendered frames are not scanned for other platforms' marks or contact details** [model+map]
   Map nodes: short.no-foreign-watermark, comp.offsite-leads — source: 抖音社区自律公约 "大段内容带有其他平台水印logo", "联系方式、二维码、链接"
   Why: stage-montage/revise.md step 4 crops known watermarks, but only when fixing after review. The real-screenshot rule (video.md 6) and the review list in `review.md` never mention logos, QR codes or handles. Grep terms: 水印, watermark, 二维码, 联系方式, logo.
   Fix: add this item to the review sheet checks in `references/review.md` and the stage-montage step 10 review.

### Minor
1. **Subtitle text is not proofread after rendering** [model+map]
   Map nodes: captions.edit, captions.accuracy-accessibility — source: YouTube Help "Edit or remove captions"; Video Production Course syllabus "M14 caption accuracy"
   Why: voice-over 3 transcribes the voice files and compares them with the script, but subtitles are only "对齐"; nothing compares on-screen subtitle text to the narration after the build.
2. **Script claims are not re-checked at render time** [map]
   Map node: comp.fact-check — source: O*NET 27-3041.00 "Verify facts, dates, and statistics"
   Why: only on-screen numbers are checked ("屏上的数字必须在脚本或调研报告里找得到"); rule 1 of 内容依据 removes unsupported claims but gives no check step.
3. **Hook payoff and loop** [map]
   Map node: short.honest-loop-payoff — source: 抖音规则中心「诱导互动」 "建立不恰当的预期吸引观看"
   Why: the ending rule ("结尾落在一个动作上") is there, but no review item asks whether the hook is delivered.
4. **Font and trademark use in graphics** [map]
   Map nodes: assets.font-licensing, assets.trademarks — source: 哔哩哔哩《UP主创作手册 通用篇》"注意字体版权问题"; 创作学院 "商标知识小课堂"
   Why: grep 字体, font, 商标 finds no licensing instruction; the skill allows naming real brands on purpose.

## Unsourced findings
- No source has been checked for these (model only).
- Several steps need macOS (`say`, Vision pose in stage-montage, whisper path `/opt/homebrew`), but make-video does not say what to do on another system beyond voice-over's "no voice" fallback. For the montage there is no fallback.
- `notes` lists voice, music source and materials, but not an explicit list of what needs the user's AI or staged-content label (synthetic voice, drawn presenter), although the skill hands that work to the user.
