# Blindspot report: claude-youtube-editor (nine sub-skills as one pipeline)

Map: video-long 0.2.1 (extends video 0.2.1, 139 nodes)    Profile: none    Date: 2026-11-17

Entry choice: I ran `prepare` on `packaging` and on `clean-cut` (the two ends of the pipeline) and read all nine `SKILL.md` files plus the five reference files by hand. The audit treats the nine as one pipeline. The repository root (`CLAUDE.md`, `README.md`, `brand.md`, `tools/`, `videos/`, `docs/`) was searched with `grep` for every Needed finding. `prepare` reports five missing files for clean-cut (`keyterms.txt`, `qa-report.md`, `review.md`, `verify-report.md`); they are per-project outputs, not shipped files, so I did not count them as gaps.

Note on order: I printed the node headings before writing the step 2 list, so the Ask list below is not fully blind to the map. Findings 1 and 2 were already visible to me from reading the skills, because three skills point at a "final-mix step" and a final assemble.

## Scope
- Work: turns raw talking-head clips into a long-form YouTube video. Steps are cut, Remotion TSX beats, voice cleanup, SFX plan, title and thumbnail packaging, and a private-draft upload via `tools/yt_upload.py`.
- Starts at raw footage in `videos/<p>/` and ends at a private YouTube draft that the creator publishes in Studio (`yt_upload.py` header: "uploads land as PRIVATE drafts").
- Does not involve: topic or script writing (README: "No script writer"), filming, public publishing, promotion, post-publish analytics (only the CTR calibration worksheet), Shorts from long videos (`docs/shorts-factory-plan.md` is a separate workflow).

## Findings

### Needed
1. **No owner for music and the final mix (loudness, ducking, true peak)** [model+map+trace]
   Map node: sound.mix — source: Video Production Course Online syllabus, "M10 an audio mix"; sound.music-selection — source: O*NET 31.0, 27-4032.00 task "Determine the specific audio and visual effects and music necessary to complete films."
   Why: `clean-audio` line 54 says "Final loudness to -14 LUFS is the final-mix step's job", `suggest-sfx` line 150 says "No music bed here — that is the final-mix step", and `tools/mix_music.py` says polish is "/assemble's job later". `grep` terms on the skill folder and repo root: `assemble`, `final-mix`, `LUFS`, `loudnorm`, `music`, `ebur128`. No skill, no `tools/assemble*` file and no `loudnorm` call exists. `tools/make_stems.py` only writes separate stems ("Nothing is ducked here ... ducking is the final mix's call") and a `video-4k-final.mp4` with no music (line 337). Scope check: the work happens inside the pipeline (step 4 sound), not after it. Trace break: three skills hand off to a step that does not exist.
   Fix: add a final-mix step after `suggest-sfx` (a section in it or a new skill) that picks the bed from `media/library/music/`, ducks it under voice and SFX, normalises to the target loudness with a true-peak ceiling, and checks the result with numbers.

2. **No final assemble, export, or pre-upload check of the delivered file** [model+map+trace]
   Map node: asm.platform-standards — source: O*NET 31.0, 27-2012.00 task "Review film, recordings, or rehearsals to ensure conformance to production and broadcast standards"; asm.review-revision — source: O*NET 27-4032.00 task "Review assembled films or edited videotapes on screens or monitors"; pub.export — source: Video Production Course syllabus, "M10 Export"; pub.upload — source: YouTube for Creators: Longform, "Upload & share in YouTube Studio"; pub.precheck (platform self-check after upload)
   Why: `make-tsx` ends at "preview re-baked, composited frames spot-checked" (1080p30). The 4K60 final and the H.264 transcode are in `clean-cut` step 10, but only for the cut master. `README.md` line 23 says upload "checks itself for ghost speech + A/V drift". `grep` terms `verify`, `ffprobe`, `drift`, `ghost`, `duration`, `final render`, `pre-upload` on `tools/yt_upload.py` return nothing about that; its checks are title, description, tags and thumbnail size only. `verify_cut.py` is tied to the cut render. The upload row in `CLAUDE.md` (line 17) has no skill, and the `publish.json` fields are only in the tool header. Scope check: export and upload are the pipeline's last step, inside scope.
   Fix: add a closing stage (after the final mix) that renders the composited final, runs the A/V duration, loudness and ghost-speech checks on that exact file, has the creator watch it end to end, then documents the `publish.json` fields for `yt_upload.py` and the Studio checks to read after upload.

### Worth considering
3. **Upload metadata guidance: tags, category, playlist, search wording, auto-chapters** [model+map]
   Map nodes: pack.tags, pack.playlists, pub.discoverability, pack.title-type (shallow), long.auto-chapters — sources: YouTube for Creators, "Make your content discoverable"; YouTube Help chapters guidance.
   Why: `yt_upload.py` accepts tags and category but `packaging` says nothing on how to choose them, on playlists, or on whether the title should match a search or intrigue. Title Checklist rule 7 only asks for "a specific noun".
   Fix: add a metadata stage to `packaging` Stage 3 that sets tags, category, playlist and the search-versus-curiosity title choice, and says whether to allow automatic chapters.

4. **Captions or subtitle track for long-form** [model+map]
   Map nodes: captions.create, captions.edit, captions.translate, captions.reach, captions.accuracy-accessibility, captions.silent-viewing — source: YouTube Help "Add subtitles & captions", "Create subtitles & captions".
   Why: `brand.md` line 110 only says "Captions: overlays only (not word-level captions) for long-form". The word-level `edited-transcript.json` exists but nothing turns it into an uploadable caption file, and `yt_upload.py` does not upload one. Searched: `caption`, `subtitle`, `srt`, `vtt`.
   Fix: add a step to the cut handoff or upload stage that writes an SRT from `edited-transcript.json` and decides whether to upload it, with a check on mis-heard keyterms.

5. **End screen room, info cards and the opening or close** [model+map]
   Map nodes: long.end-screen, long.end-screen-timing, long.info-cards, long.open-close-key-moments (shallow) — sources: YouTube Help "Add an end screen", "Best practices for creating end screens"; "Add cards to a video".
   Why: `grep` for `end screen`, `endscreen`, `info card` finds nothing in skills or `brand.md` (only unrelated "cards" shots). Videos end with a custom outro shot (`B15CloseOutro`), and nothing says to leave 5-20 seconds for Studio end elements.
   Fix: add a line to `make-tsx` (outro beat) on end-screen length and clear space, and to the upload stage on adding end screen and cards in Studio.

6. **Disclosure of AI-generated and simulated content** [model+map]
   Map nodes: comp.ai-disclosure, comp.staged-content (shallow) — sources: Video Production Course syllabus "M14 Use AI responsibly".
   Why: `make-tsx` review-rules §2 rule 7 requires "AI-generated images", `fake-screencast` builds simulated recordings, and `docs/ai-clone-guide.md` covers an AI clone. `grep` terms `disclos`, `synthetic`, `altered`, `label` across the repo find nothing about telling viewers or setting a flag. `yt_upload.py` sets `selfDeclaredMadeForKids` but no content-label field.
   Fix: add to `fake-screencast` and the upload stage a rule on when to say a walkthrough is simulated and when a synthetic-content label is needed.

7. **Rights check for third-party logos, page captures and music** [model+map]
   Map nodes: assets.rights-acquisition, assets.copyright-fair-use, assets.trademarks, assets.music-licensing (shallow) — source: Video Production Course syllabus (rights and clearances).
   Why: `make-tsx` P3 requires real logos (review-rules: "real logos") and real-page clones and cloned third-party pages; `suggest-sfx` records `license` for SFX only. `grep` `copyright`, `fair use`, `trademark`, `licen` in skills finds only the SFX line.
   Fix: add a short rights note to `make-tsx` (logos and captured pages) and make the music catalog `license` check part of the final-mix step.

9. **Backup of raw footage and masters** [model+map] — node pub.archive (shallow). `CLAUDE.md` says raw footage and master cuts "NEVER go to git" but names no backup.
10. **Sponsored segments** [map] — nodes comp.sponsor-disclosure, long.sponsor-integration, long.sponsor-amount-position (source: YouTube Help "paid promotion button"). No sponsor handling anywhere (`grep sponsor` is empty); depends on whether the channel takes sponsors.
11. **Honest-review and engagement checks inside the video** [map] — nodes comp.honest-review, comp.engagement-bait (both shallow). Packaging has a promise-keeping guardrail; nothing checks in-video prompts or tool claims.
12. **File-size and length limits at upload** [map] — node long.upload-limits (shallow). `yt_upload.py` validates metadata only.
