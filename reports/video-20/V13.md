# Blindspot report: video-explainer (claude-video-kit) (V13)

Map: video-long 0.2.1 (extends video), combined with base map; Profile: none    Date: 2026-11-17

Audit target: runesleo/claude-video-kit, skills/video-explainer/SKILL.md plus its six references and docs/RELEASE_GATE.md (named by the skill). Scripts and tests were not run. The skill makes vertical 9:16 explainers; video-long was applied as instructed, and nodes that are specific to horizontal YouTube long-form (end screens, info cards) were marked not-applicable for that reason.

## Scope
- Takes a research brief, finished script or generated MP4 and produces a reviewed, objectively verified vertical (9:16) explainer, with three production strategies (direct model output, agent-orchestrated, deterministic Remotion fallback).
- Includes a pre-render review, final-artifact review bound to the MP4 hash, and a platform release gate (loudness, cover size, QA files). Ends at a verified local MP4 plus an evidence packet.
- Never uploads, signs in, pays or posts. Does not research the topic (brief is input) and does not film.

## Findings

### Needed
1. **No AI-content disclosure although the skill can output a fully model-generated video** [model+map]
   Map node: comp.ai-disclosure — source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M14 Use AI responsibly"
   Why: Strategy `direct_artifact` has a model produce the final MP4, and the legacy path uses synthetic voices. The twelve review checks and the release gate include privacy and copyright but not a platform AI/synthetic label or its wording. Searched "AI-generated", "synthetic", "label": no match.

### Worth considering
2. **Direct and agent-orchestrated paths skip the source-bound facts rule** [trace]
   Map node: comp.fact-check — source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M14 verifying AI-drafted facts"
   Why: "Make every number, date, attribution ... reviewable from the supplied source" lives in brief-to-script.md, which is only read for `legacy_remotion`. A `direct_artifact` MP4 meets facts only at the final review, with no claim list to check against.
3. **Cover/subtitle/audio edits are required before the gate, but no step makes them on the direct path** [trace+model]
   Map node: captions.edit, pack.thumbnail — source: YouTube Help: Edit or remove captions, "Edit caption text"
   Why: Step 8: "finish all cover/subtitle/audio edits", yet SKILL.md never names how a cover or subtitle file is made on any path (scripts such as prepend-cover.sh exist but are not referenced from the skill).
4. **No title, description, tags or chapters** [model+map]
   Map node: pack.title, pack.title-type, pack.tags, pack.description-chapters, pub.discoverability, long.chapters — source: YouTube Help: Thumbnail & title tips, "How to write titles"
   Why: "Keep upload and platform-specific publishing copy outside script.json." A handoff pack exists in the repo, but the skill itself does not say how titles or descriptions are made or checked.
5. **No music/SFX stance, voice licence or sponsor disclosure** [model+map]
   Map node: sound.music-selection, sound.effects, assets.music-licensing, comp.sponsor-disclosure, comp.community-guidelines, comp.advertiser-friendly — source: YouTube for Creators: Shorts, "Choose your music"
   Why: "Private voices" are kept out, but nothing checks the licence of the TTS voice or any music bed (docs/bgm-library-and-mux-flow.md exists but is not referenced).

### Minor
6. **Remaining absent or shallow nodes, not individually argued** [map]
   Nodes: topic.focus, script.audience-retention, assets.rights-acquisition, assets.copyright-fair-use, assets.font-licensing, assets.generative-material, assets.trademarks, edit.rough-cut, captions.translate, captions.reach, captions.accuracy-accessibility, captions.silent-viewing, asm.timecode-check, pub.archive, comp.copyright-strike, comp.media-law-ethics, comp.submission-rules, comp.violation-categories, comp.engagement-bait, long.end-screen-timing
   Why: no matching instruction in the skill; each is either outside what an author of this skill is likely to add, or a small addition. Exclude in a profile if intentional.
