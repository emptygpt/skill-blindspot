## Scope
- Turns a given video (mp4/mov/mkv/webm) into a Chinese narrated recap or an original-sound cut: understanding, story plan, shot selection, narration, TTS, mix, render; can export a Jianying draft.
- Responsibility ends at the rendered MP4, subtitles, cover.jpg and optional promo/packaging notes; upload and distribution are explicitly operator work (docs/production-boundaries.md). The sibling skill video-reference only analyses finished films.
- Does not involve filming, hosting, or live content. Reads: video-recap SKILL.md, its references/ (creative-editing-playbook, promotional-copy, resource-library, audio-modes, config-playbook, research-guide, packaging, shot-review, data-schema skimmed), video-reference SKILL.md, docs/production-boundaries.md.

## Findings

### Needed
1. **Rights to the source footage and the originality of a recap** [model+map]
   Map nodes: assets.rights-acquisition, assets.copyright-fair-use, comp.originality-reposts, comp.declare-original-repost, comp.copyright-strike, comp.screen-recording-original, pre.releases
   Why: The whole product re-cuts someone else's film. The only trace is resource-library.md ("版权或声音授权未确认" warning when licence status is unknown) and no instruction to obtain permission, judge transformative use, or declare original versus repost. Searched 版权/授权/侵权/二创 in SKILL.md and all references.
2. **Disclosure of AI-synthetic narration** [model+map]
   Map nodes: comp.ai-disclosure
   Why: Narration is TTS and may use a cloned voice (voice-clone consent is covered), but nothing tells the agent to label AI-made voice or content at upload. Searched AI生成/声明/标注.

### Worth considering
1. **Platform rules and content-risk check before release** [model+map]
   Map nodes: comp.community-guidelines, comp.submission-rules, comp.violation-categories, comp.advertiser-friendly, comp.media-law-ethics, comp.minors, comp.dangerous-content, asm.platform-standards
   Why: final_qc.json checks decode, duration and spec only. No check against platform content rules, ad-friendliness or minors/violence in the source scenes.
2. **Long-video structure for publishing: chapters, end screen room** [model+map]
   Map nodes: long.chapters, long.end-screen, long.end-screen-timing, pack.description-chapters
   Why: Target durations reach 3-10 minutes, but no chapter list, end-screen room or description chapters are produced; promo_copy has titles, cover, description and tags only.
3. **Cost control for paid API calls** [model+map]
   Map nodes: pre.budget
   Why: ASR, VLM and TTS are paid remote calls; README quotes cost but SKILL.md gives no estimate or cap step before running them.
4. **Export presets and project archive** [map]
   Map nodes: pub.export, pub.archive, pub.one-to-many
   Why: One MP4 spec; no platform presets, no archive step, no second-ratio cut.
