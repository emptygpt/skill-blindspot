# Blindspot report: vincentsch/explainroo (skills/explainroo)

Map: video-long 0.2.1 (extends video 0.2.1)    Profile: none    Date: 2026-11-17

## Scope
- Local pipeline for explainer, tutorial, launch and short videos with synthetic or own-recorded narration, drawn scenes (HTML/Chrome), music and effects, rendered to MP4 with ffmpeg, then packaged per platform and optionally posted (YouTube, Buffer, Blotato).
- Starts at a topic from the user; ends at a finished video, subtitles, chapters, cover and a package folder, with an optional upload. It is a tool with ~25 topic docs; SKILL.md tells the agent which doc to read at each step. SKILL.md and docs/ were read; src/ code was not run.
- Not for filmed footage ("something explainroo cannot do, like video shot with a camera"); post-publish analytics and channel strategy are outside the flow.
- Default length 45-120 s; also has a short-video kit and a 16:9 YouTube size with chapters, so the long-form map is applied, with long nodes aimed at multi-minute videos judged against that default.

## Findings

### Needed
1. **Narration facts are not verified after writing; only audio and layout are checked** [model+map]
   Map node: comp.fact-check - source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M14 verifying AI-drafted facts"; comp.media-law-ethics - source: Video: Digital Communication & Production, 5th edition (2023), "Ch3 Media Law / Media Ethics"; comp.community-guidelines - source: YouTube for Creators: Helpful Resources, "Community Guidelines" (also: comp.copyright-strike, comp.originality-reposts, assets.copyright-fair-use, assets.rights-acquisition, comp.honest-review, comp.submission-rules)
   Why: "Never make up numbers, quotes or claims" and "write down the facts you will use" exist, but `check` and `verify` test timing, loudness, layout and whether words were spoken as written, not whether the claims are right. No step compares the finished narration with the noted facts or sources before `package` or `publish`.

### Worth considering
1. **Audience and goal are never written down; no definition of what the video must achieve** [model+map]
   Map node: topic.focus - source: YouTube for Creators: Longform, "Find your focus"; topic.project-definition - source: Video: Digital Communication & Production, 5th edition (2023), "Ch13 Defining the Project"; topic.audience-research - source: YouTube for Creators: Understand your audience, "Use data to understand your audience" (also: script.revision, asm.review-revision)
   Why: Searched "audience", "goal", "who it is for": only "Pick the look for the audience". Review is by the agent looking at frames; there is no feedback round with the user before packaging.
2. **YouTube end screens, cards and call to action not addressed** [model+map]
   Map node: long.end-screen - source: YouTube Help: Add end screens to videos, "Add an end screen"; long.end-screen-timing - source: YouTube Help: Add end screens to videos, "Change element timing"; long.info-cards - source: YouTube Help: Add info cards to videos, "Add cards to a video" (also: long.auto-chapters, long.clips-from-long)
   Why: The long form is packaged for YouTube with chapters and cover, but nothing leaves room for end screens or cards, or tells the agent to decide on a call to action; writing.md even says not to tell viewers to do obvious things.
3. **16:9 videos get no burned-in captions by default; caption proofreading and accessibility are thin** [map]
   Map node: captions.silent-viewing - source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M13 captions-on viewing"; captions.edit - source: YouTube Help: Edit or remove captions, "Edit caption text"; captions.accuracy-accessibility - source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M14 caption accuracy for accessibility" (also: captions.reach, edit.colour)
   Why: "a 16:9 video gets none" with `"captions": "auto"`; .srt is produced for upload; no step to proofread caption text.
4. **Titles, tags, publish timing and discoverability get little guidance** [map]
   Map node: pack.title - source: YouTube Help: Thumbnail & title tips, "How to write titles"; pack.title-type - source: YouTube Help: Thumbnail & title tips, "Types of video titles"; pack.tags - source: 哔哩哔哩《UP主创作手册 通用篇》(2020), "3 标签" (also: pub.discoverability, pub.publish-time, pub.scheduled-publish, pub.precheck, pub.archive)
   Why: `post.json` carries a title and hashtags with length checks only; no advice on choosing them, and no scheduler.
