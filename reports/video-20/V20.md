# Blindspot report: V20 video-factory studio (flow v5 + sub-skills)

Map: video 0.2.1    Profile: none    Date: 2026-11-17

## Scope
- A studio entry skill (plus onboarding, story-architect, cast, cinematographer, references, format-series, format-longform) that runs an enforced flow: seven-question intake, series bible, concept, script, cast, radio cut/storyboard, live prices, Production Book with budget lock, generation, edit, QC, delivery receipt, retro. Verticals: sitcom, micro-drama, UGC ad, AI influencer, podcast clip, anime, cinematic, fantasy, launch film, long-form meditation.
- Responsibility ends at a delivered MP4 with receipt and a retro; only the long-form recipe also produces title, description with chapters and thumbnail. No upload step.
- Not involved: filming real footage; channel operation. Engine code (engine/*.mjs) was not read; behaviours are taken from the skill text, and a claim like "the engine enforces" is counted as stated, not verified.

## Findings

### Needed
1. **AI-made people, voices and reviews are never labelled or disclosed outside one vertical** [model+map]
   Map nodes: comp.ai-disclosure, comp.staged-content, comp.honest-review, comp.sponsor-disclosure, comp.community-guidelines
   Why: The factory makes AI influencers, "clone me", UGC "creator-style review" ads and photoreal micro-dramas. Only meditation-longform.md says "realistic synthetic content must be disclosed". UGC ads, influencer and micro-drama verticals do not mention platform AI labels, #ad/paid disclosure, or the rule not to present AI reviewers as real users. Searched disclos, synthetic, sponsor, label, FTC across the skills and README.
2. **Music and provider-asset licensing is not addressed** [model+map]
   Map nodes: assets.music-licensing, assets.rights-acquisition, comp.copyright-strike
   Why: Scores, beds and stings are generated or picked via provider tools (musicBrief, ACE) and delivered for platforms, but no line says what use rights apply or what to do on a copyright claim.

### Worth considering
1. **Packaging for formats other than long-form** [model+map]
   Map nodes: pack.thumbnail, pack.title, pack.tags, pack.description-chapters, pack.title-type, pub.discoverability
   Why: Title, description and thumbnail appear only in format-longform and the meditation DNA; sitcom, micro-drama, UGC and anime end at "send the mp4".
2. **Captions: only micro-drama states them** [model+map]
   Map nodes: captions.create, captions.edit, captions.accuracy-accessibility, captions.translate, captions.reach
   Why: "Captions | on, synced to the final track" is the only caption line; "creative calls are the creator's" means captions are not added unless asked.
3. **Audience data does not enter or leave the flow** [model+map+trace]
   Map nodes: topic.audience-research, ana.insights, topic.subject-research
   Why: Retro writes production lessons; no step collects post-release data, and the intake never asks for audience evidence outside the launch film ICP. Trace: retro output (DNA file) is not fed by data.
4. **Platform submission and minors rules** [map]
   Map nodes: comp.submission-rules, comp.violation-categories, comp.minors, comp.advertiser-friendly, comp.media-law-ethics
   Why: Only the meditation vertical lists YouTube policy; other formats have no platform rule check before delivery.
