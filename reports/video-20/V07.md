# Blindspot report: fendouai/ViralForge (skills/viralforge)

Map: video-short 0.2.1 (extends video 0.2.1)    Profile: none    Date: 2026-11-17

## Scope
- Analyses a viral short reference video, produces a structural "DNA" report, and plans a replica (same structure, new product, script, voice, avatar) plus 10 costed variants.
- Starts at a reference video the user owns or may analyse; ends at a DNA report, a VFML project skeleton and a variant list with costs. Final generation runs on a separate cloud service ("Generation models ... are cloud capabilities (WearAI)").
- Does not publish, upload, write titles/covers, or measure results; no filming.
- The analysis engine (`viralforge analyze`, `scripts/analyze.py`, MCP server) is code that was not run or audited here; claims about what it measures are taken from SKILL.md.

## Findings

### Needed
1. **Generation and checking of the replica and variants are not described; costs have no stated source** [trace+model+map]
   Map node: edit.workflow - source: YouTube for Creators: Longform, "Edit your video"; edit.rough-cut - source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M10 Assemble one scene"; short.ai-tools - source: YouTube for Creators: Shorts, "Dream Track" (also: short.quality-floor, asm.review-revision, edit.principles)
   Why: Steps 4-5 say "Replicate" and "Generate 10 versions" and "Record cost per variant", but the open engine "covers deconstruction and composition" and generation is a cloud capability; no step says how to call it, where prices come from, or how generated shots are reviewed before a variant is accepted. Outputs reach the label step with only "Never claim a render ... that did not happen" as a check.
2. **Replica is a product promotion but no claim check, sponsorship or duplicate-upload guidance** [model+map]
   Map node: topic.subject-research - source: O*NET 31.0 Database, task statements for video-related occupations, "27-2012.00 task: Research production topics using the internet, video archives, and other informational sources."; comp.sponsor-disclosure - source: Disclosures 101 for Social Media Influencers (November 2019), "How to Disclose"; comp.no-duplicate-upload - source: 帮助中心「投稿规则 > 投稿规范」(对稿件内容的要求/非自制内容范围/对稿件的其他规则/对稿件信息的要求), "撞车及重复投稿规则" (also: short.no-bulk-posting, comp.staged-content, comp.fact-check)
   Why: Searched "fact", "claim", "sponsor", "disclos": only the AI label appears. Selling points are put into a copied hook structure with no verification, and ten near-identical variants are created without a warning about posting similar videos on one or several accounts.
