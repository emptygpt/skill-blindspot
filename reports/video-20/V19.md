# Blindspot report: V19 director-skills (miora-video-studio, seedance-motion-studio, travel-skill + 4 prompt skills)

Map: video 0.2.1    Profile: none    Date: 2026-11-17

## Scope
- Seven director skills for AI video: seedance-motion-studio is the end-to-end process (brief, research, directions, beats, assets, shot cards, pilot, generate, select, edit notes, QA, handoff); travel-skill and travel-photo-to-video do stills-to-clip planning; miora-video-studio adds parameter gates and polling for the Miora tool; cinematic-asset-prompts, action-fight-prompt and emotion-performance-director write prompts only.
- Responsibility ends at prompts, generated candidates, edit/post notes and a handoff report; the skills say they cannot assemble or publish the final film themselves and do not run Seedance unless a verified route exists. Audited as one pipeline: seedance-motion-studio (spine), miora-video-studio (the entry named in the list), travel-skill; the other four read at description level.
- Not involved: filming, uploading, account work.

## Findings

### Needed
1. **No permission or IP gate for real people, real photos and referenced styles** [model+map]
   Map nodes: pre.releases, assets.rights-acquisition, assets.copyright-fair-use, comp.media-law-ethics
   Why: travel-skill only gives "明确风险提醒，不擅自替用户判定已获授权"; seedance asks for reference IDs but not for who owns or permits them; no instruction to stop when a real person's photo, a famous character or a living artist's style is used. Searched 授权, 肖像, 版权, likeness, copyright across the seven skills.
2. **No platform AI-content label or content-safety check on generated clips** [model+map]
   Map nodes: comp.ai-disclosure, comp.minors, comp.dangerous-content, comp.community-guidelines
   Why: Only "clearly label it as illustrative" for evidence-like imagery. Nothing on platform AI labels, or on checking generated fights (action-fight-prompt), minors or real-person depictions against platform rules before delivery.

### Worth considering
1. **Captions are a delivery format item only** [model+map]
   Map nodes: captions.transcription, captions.edit, captions.translate, captions.accuracy-accessibility, captions.silent-viewing, captions.reach
   Why: postproduction.md lists "字幕形式" and "字幕与旁白稿" but gives no way to produce, check or time captions; AI dialogue cannot be controlled (miora 3.4).
2. **Title, cover and publishing metadata** [model+map]
   Map nodes: pack.thumbnail, pack.title, pack.tags, pack.description-chapters, pack.title-type
   Why: Delivery ends at the file; brand promos and travel films have no packaging step.
3. **Loudness and audio cleanup** [model]
   Map nodes: sound.mix, sound.ai-cleanup
   Why: Audio quality gate says "对白清晰、音效不过载" with no loudness target and no repair route.
4. **Sponsor and advertising rules for brand/travel promos** [model+map]
   Map nodes: comp.sponsor-disclosure, comp.advertiser-friendly, comp.submission-rules
   Why: brand-promo and travel modules produce promotional films with no ad-disclosure or platform ad rule step.
