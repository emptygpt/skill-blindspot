# Blindspot report: make-short (claude-faceless-shorts-creator)

Map: video-short v0.2.1 (extends video v0.2.1)    Profile: none    Date: 2026-11-17

Skill under audit: hassancs91/claude-faceless-shorts-creator, .claude/skills/make-short/SKILL.md, with CLAUDE.md, brand.md, IDEAS.md (read); tools/ scripts not read (code, untrusted). Sub-skills vidtsx-2d-generator and suggest-sfx are only named, not audited.

## Scope
- Fully synthetic vertical short (1080x1920, ~40 s): script and beats.json, Remotion TSX composition, QA frames, ElevenLabs voice with word-timed captions, SFX mix, optional music bed.
- Responsibility ends at a voiced, SFX-mixed mp4 "awaiting the user's ear" (Done = list). No upload, scheduling or analytics.
- No filming, no stock footage, no people on camera ("no footage, no stock"). Other tracks (make-ai-short, make-vox) are out of scope.

8. Caption accessibility, language variants, flashing-content check: not found.

## Findings

### Needed
1. **Fact verification has no method, record or gate** [model+map+trace]
   Map node: comp.fact-check, topic.subject-research, script.ai-assisted — source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M14 verifying AI-drafted facts"
   Why: Searched for sources, a fact log or a check before render. Only "Facts must be verified before scripting." and "(facts verified)" in Done. Nothing is produced that a later step or a person could check; AI-drafted facts reach the voice track unchecked.
2. **Post-voice re-render and final mixed audio are never checked** [model+trace]
   Map node: asm.review-revision, sound.mix — source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M10 review-and-revision workflow"
   Why: Stage 3 QA runs before voice. Stage 4 says "re-render the composition (captions retimed) and mux" with no QA stage after it; the only audio target is "~−15.5 LUFS" for the SFX mix. A caption or timing break introduced by new word times would be seen first by the viewer.
3. **Synthetic voice, music and SFX rights and AI labelling are not addressed** [model+map]
   Map node: comp.ai-disclosure, assets.music-licensing, short.music-eligibility, assets.copyright-fair-use — source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M14 Use AI responsibly"
   Why: Searched for licence, terms, disclosure, label. The skill generates voice, SFX and music (gen_voice, gen_sfx, gen_music) and ends at a postable file without mentioning commercial-use terms or platform labels for synthetic media.

### Worth considering
1. **Title, description, hashtags and posting copy are not produced** [model+map]
   Map node: pack.title, pack.title-type, pack.tags — source: YouTube Help: Thumbnail & title tips, "How to write titles"
   Why: Done = lists only the mp4 and plan files. Nothing writes the text that goes with the upload.
2. **Seamless loop design conflicts with a platform rule** [map+trace]
   Map node: short.honest-loop-payoff — source: 抖音规则中心 规则文件「诱导互动」, "首尾内容完全一致，让人不易觉察循环播放"
   Why: The skill requires a last frame equal to frame 0 so the loop is not noticed; Douyin lists identical start and end hiding the loop as unwelcome. Depends on the target platform, which the skill never names.
3. **Topic choice has no audience, trend or success check** [model+map]
   Map node: topic.audience-research, topic.trends-evergreen, topic.success-definition, topic.project-definition, short.trending-inspiration — source: YouTube for Creators: Understand your audience, "Use data to understand your audience"
   Why: Topics come from an idea bank filtered by "does TSX win", i.e. by format fit. No demand, search or performance signal, and no definition of a hit.
4. **No platform spec or duration-limit check** [model+map]
   Map node: asm.platform-standards, pub.export, short.duration-limit — source: O*NET 31.0 Database, task statements for video-related occupations, "27-2012.00 task: Review film, recordings, or rehearsals to ensure conf"
   Why: Only a frame size, a bottom safe zone and AAC settings are given; no platform duration cap, bitrate or codec requirement.
5. **Caption language, accessibility and cover are thin** [model+map]
   Map node: captions.translate, captions.accuracy-accessibility, captions.silent-viewing, short.cover, pack.thumbnail — source: YouTube for Creators: Understand your audience, "Add captions and translations"
   Why: English-only captions, no contrast check, no named cover frame or text.
6. **No step to revise the script after review** [map]
   Map node: script.revision — source: ESCO classification v1.2.1, skills of video-related occupations, "edit scripts"
   Why: Script is written once in Stage 1; later stages only change the composition and voice.
