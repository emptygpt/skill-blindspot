Map: video 0.2.1    Profile: none    Date: 2026-11-17

## Scope
- Turns a brief into a short explainer MP4 (or other routes: template-based sessions, single tools) with voiceover, generated visuals, captions, build and QC; optional YouTube upload with subtitles, chapters and synthetic-media flag.
- Reads: skills/video-toolkit/SKILL.md, its referenced docs (docs/youtube-upload.md) and, as the pipeline's own files, .claude/skills/video-design, youtube-stats, and .claude/commands/publish.md and thumbnail.md. Evidence found only in command files is marked shallow, because SKILL.md does not reference them.
- Responsibility runs from brief to upload (private by default); channel strategy and monetisation are not covered. No camera shoot: visuals are generated, rendered or screen-recorded.
- Code in tools/ not read (data only).

## Findings

### Needed
1. **No research or fact-check of what the model narrates** [model+map+trace]
   Map nodes: topic.subject-research, comp.fact-check, topic.audience-research
   Why: The job route makes the agent write the narration from a brief and then voice, illustrate and QC it. qc.py checks audio, timing and layout; copy_lint checks style ("it's worth noting", dashes). Nothing checks facts, figures or sources before spend or before upload. Trace: the spec is the only content artifact and nothing downstream validates its claims.
2. **Consent and rights for cloned voices, people's photos and generated likenesses** [model+map]
   Map nodes: pre.releases, assets.rights-acquisition, assets.copyright-fair-use, assets.trademarks, assets.font-licensing
   Why: /voice-clone, kits "their own photos" and talking-head tools (SoulX) animate real people and clone voices; searched for consent, permission, likeness in SKILL.md, kits/README.md, commands: no step requires permission from the person. Music/sfx licence handling is covered; images, fonts, logos and clips are not.

### Worth considering
1. **Script approval before paid generation on the job route** [model+trace]
   Map nodes: script.revision
   Why: The job route submits a model-written spec under a cost cap; the session route says review points go back to the person, the job route does not. A wrong script is paid for before anyone sees it.
2. **Platform policy and advertiser checks before upload** [model+map]
   Map nodes: comp.community-guidelines, comp.advertiser-friendly, comp.copyright-strike, comp.originality-reposts, comp.media-law-ethics, comp.minors, comp.dangerous-content, comp.sponsor-disclosure
   Why: The only compliance item is the synthetic-media flag. No made-for-kids, advertiser-friendly, copyright-claim or sponsor disclosure step.
3. **Close the loop: stats and A/B tests feed the next video** [model+map+trace]
   Map nodes: ana.ab-test, ana.early-ctr, ana.case-teardown, ana.refresh-old, ana.community
   Why: youtube-stats exists but the entry skill never calls it, and nothing feeds topic choice or thumbnail/title tests. Output of stats is not used by any step.
4. **Titles and thumbnails live in command files** [map]
   Map nodes: pack.title-type, pack.thumbnail, pack.title, pack.tags
   Why: Present in /publish and /thumbnail but not referenced by SKILL.md, so a job-route agent never sees them.
