# Blindspot report: claude-youtube-editor (9 sub-skills as one pipeline) (V11)

Map: video-long 0.2.1 (extends video), combined with base map; Profile: none    Date: 2026-11-17

Audit target: hassancs91/claude-youtube-editor, .claude/skills/ (clean-cut, clean-audio, make-tsx, fake-screencast, vidtsx-2d-generator, suggest-sfx, packaging, thumbnail, brand-setup) plus the repo CLAUDE.md and brand.md and the references the skills name. Pipeline order given in the repo: cut, visuals, voice, SFX, packaging, upload.

## Scope
- Post-production of a recorded talking-head YouTube long video: transcript-driven cut, Remotion screen beats, voice cleanup, SFX plan, title and thumbnail packaging, then a private YouTube draft upload (tool named only in CLAUDE.md).
- Starts at raw footage and an edit plan; does not choose topics, write scripts, or film. Ends at a private draft on YouTube; no publishing, promotion or channel operation.
- Long-form only ("If asked to package a Short, decline"). Files reviewed are skills, two references and brand.md; the Python/TS tools were not run or read in depth.

## Step 2 list (written before the map was opened)
Searched the nine SKILL.md files, CLAUDE.md and brand.md for each item before opening the map.
1. Final audio mix (music, loudness target, true peak) as an actual step. Found only "Final loudness to -14 LUFS is the final-mix step's job" in clean-audio and brand.md pointers; no skill for it.
2. Final composited render/export of the finished video with SFX and music. Found `bake.py` producing a 1080p30 preview; the "final-mix step" is referenced, not defined.
3. Closed captions or subtitle track for long-form. brand.md says "Captions: overlays only (not word-level captions) for long-form."
4. Accessibility checks beyond colour contrast in brand-setup.
5. End screens and info cards. No match for "end screen" or "cards".
6. Disclosure of AI-generated or synthetic content (AI-generated thumbnails of the creator's face, "Hero objects are AI-generated images", simulated screencasts). No match for "disclos" or "synthetic".
7. Rights for third-party material shown in beats (real logos, cloned pages of real services). Only SFX catalog records license.
8. Fact check of claims shown on screen. Found only "Real service info -> real page (P3)".
9. A hook/opening review of the assembled cut. Found only "cutting fluff and redundancy" as a retention lever.
10. Who writes edit-plan.md. It is the "source of truth" for make-tsx; no skill produces it.
11. Feedback from retention data into the next cut. yt_stats.py exists; no skill reads it.
12. Sponsor/affiliate disclosure in description CTAs. Not found.
13. Back-up of raw footage (raw is git-ignored). Not found.
14. Title/description search keywords and tags. Title checklist exists; tags not found.
15. Platform policy self-check before upload (copyright claims, advertiser-friendly). Not found.

## Findings

### Needed
1. **The final mix and final export are referenced but no step defines them** [model+map+trace]
   Map node: sound.mix, sound.music-selection, pub.export, assets.music-licensing — source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M10 an audio mix"
   Why: clean-audio and suggest-sfx both end with "that is the final-mix step" (music bed, auto-ducking, -14 LUFS). No SKILL.md owns it; make-tsx bakes only a 1080p30 preview. The skill never says how the finished video with voice, SFX, music and beats becomes one exported file, or checks its loudness.
2. **No disclosure of AI-generated or simulated visuals** [model+map]
   Map node: comp.ai-disclosure, comp.staged-content, assets.generative-material — source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M14 Use AI responsibly"
   Why: Searched for disclos / synthetic / altered: no match. Thumbnails use a model-rendered likeness of the creator, beats use "AI-generated images", and fake-screencast builds a "simulated screen recording" of products. Nothing tells the agent to set the upload's altered-content flag or to label simulated footage.
3. **Rights for third-party logos, UI clones and music are not checked** [model+map]
   Map node: assets.rights-acquisition, assets.copyright-fair-use, assets.trademarks, comp.copyright-strike — source: O*NET 31.0 Database, task statements for video-related occupations, "27-2012.00 task: Obtain rights to scripts or to such items as existing video footage."
   Why: make-tsx asks for "real logos" and TSX clones of real service pages; no licence, fair-use or trademark step. Only the SFX catalog records licence.
4. **On-screen claims are not fact-checked** [model+map]
   Map node: comp.fact-check — source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M14 verifying AI-drafted facts"
   Why: P3 says to show a real service's limits or pricing as a page clone with the line highlighted, but nothing checks that the number matches the source on the publish date, or any claim spoken in the video.
5. **edit-plan.md is the source of truth but nothing produces it** [trace+model]
   Map node: long.open-close-key-moments — source: YouTube for Creators: Longform, "How do I edit videos for YouTube?"
   Why: make-tsx "Inputs: The plan - videos/video-N/work/edit-plan.md ... source of truth"; clean-cut calls its audit "the same as the plan step". No skill writes the plan, so the opening, close and key moments are unplanned inside this pipeline.

### Worth considering
6. **No captions or subtitle track for long-form** [model+map]
   Map node: captions.create, captions.edit, captions.translate, captions.reach, captions.accuracy-accessibility, captions.silent-viewing — source: YouTube Help: Add subtitles & captions, "Create subtitles & captions"
   Why: brand.md: "Captions: overlays only (not word-level captions) for long-form." A word-level transcript already exists (edited-transcript.json) but is not turned into a caption file for upload.
7. **No end screen or info-card design, and no room left for them** [model+map]
   Map node: long.end-screen, long.end-screen-timing, long.end-screen-metrics, long.info-cards — source: YouTube Help: Add end screens to videos, "Add an end screen"
   Why: Searched "end screen", "cards": none. The make-tsx beats can cover the last seconds where YouTube places end elements.
8. **No retention feedback into cutting or into the next cut** [model+map+trace]
   Map node: long.retention-report, long.retention-moments, long.retention-benchmark, long.retention-segments, long.retention-detail, ana.early-ctr — source: YouTube Help: Measure key moments for audience retention, "Measure key moments for audience retention"
   Why: The cut is tuned for retention ("the big retention lever"), yet no step reads retention or first-day CTR after upload. packaging calibration uses CTR only, and "Studio ... is not in the API" so it is manual.
9. **No platform-policy and sponsor-disclosure check before upload** [model+map]
   Map node: comp.community-guidelines, comp.advertiser-friendly, comp.sponsor-disclosure, comp.submission-rules, comp.violation-categories, comp.media-law-ethics, pub.precheck — source: YouTube for Creators: Helpful Resources, "Community Guidelines"
   Why: Packaging asks for a "Conversion target" (course, tool, repo) and writes CTAs; no sponsor/affiliate disclosure or YouTube policy self-check appears.
10. **No colour correction and no back-up of raw footage** [model]
   Map node: edit.colour, pub.archive — source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M10 basic color correction"
   Why: Raw footage is git-ignored by design and nothing says where it is backed up; no grading step for the 10-bit HEVC footage.

(4) The upload step is not covered by any skill, so nothing checks the draft.
