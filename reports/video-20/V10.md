# Blindspot report: iart-ai/explainer-video-skills (skills/explainer-video)

Map: video-long 0.2.1 (extends video 0.2.1)    Profile: none    Date: 2026-11-17

## Scope
- Turns one message into a 30-90 s narrated explainer: script, storyboard, scene build (Remotion or HTML), narration and captions, edit, polish, ending in a rendered `out/*.mp4`.
- Starts at an idea or feature supplied by the user; ends at the encoded MP4 (plus the project). Does not publish, title, thumbnail or measure.
- Reads SKILL.md and `references/script-to-screen-workflow.md`; helper scripts in the repo `scripts/` (contact-sheet, probe-mp4, seek-shot) are named but were not run. Sibling skills (diagram, whiteboard, isometric, wrapped) are not audited.
- Map `video-long` was chosen per the batch rule; the stated runtime (30-90 s) means chapters, platform end screens, retention and upload-limit nodes are marked not-applicable.

## Findings

### Needed
1. **No research or fact-check for the facts, stats and claims the video states** [model+map]
   Map node: topic.subject-research - source: O*NET 31.0 Database, task statements for video-related occupations, "27-2012.00 task: Research production topics using the internet, video archives, and other informational sources."; comp.fact-check - source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M14 verifying AI-drafted facts"; comp.media-law-ethics - source: Video: Digital Communication & Production, 5th edition (2023), "Ch3 Media Law / Media Ethics" (also: script.ai-assisted, topic.audience-research)
   Why: Searched "fact", "source", "verify", "research", "audience": nothing. The script is written from "one raw idea or feature" and a worked example with invented figures; the checklist ends with message, timing, captions and style, never accuracy.
2. **Audio is named but never produced, verified or mixed; scene timing is estimated, not measured** [model+map+trace]
   Map node: sound.narration-recording - source: O*NET 31.0 Database, task statements for video-related occupations, "27-4011.00 task: Perform narration of productions or present announcements."; sound.audio-editing - source: O*NET 31.0 Database, task statements for video-related occupations, "27-4011.00 task: Record and edit audio material, such as movie soundtracks, using audio recording and editing equipment."; sound.music-selection - source: YouTube for Creators: Shorts, "Choose your music" (also: sound.effects, sound.mix, script.revision)
   Why: Step 4 says "record/generate VO"; the Polish step lists "audio mix"; neither says how, with what, or at what level. Scene length is derived from `words / 2.3 + 0.4` before any audio exists, and the verify list ("renders cleanly ... narration and caption match ... plays with narration") never measures actual audio duration, loudness or clipping, and `probe-mp4.sh` checks only resolution, codec, fps and duration. If real audio is longer or shorter than the estimate, cuts and captions drift with no step to re-time.
