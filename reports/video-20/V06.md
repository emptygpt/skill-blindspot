# Blindspot report: ninestonelee/youtube-shorts-factory

Map: video-short 0.2.1 (extends video 0.2.1)    Profile: none    Date: 2026-11-17

## Scope
- Turns a reference YouTube Short into an adapted, storyboarded, AI-generated vertical story video (images, then Veo-style clips, then burned-in subtitles).
- Starts at a reference URL typed by the user; ends at `final_subtitled.mp4` in a local output folder. It does not upload, title, thumbnail or analyse the result.
- Fiction/drama content with no real people filmed; everything visual is generated, so shooting, location and equipment work do not apply.
- Depends on an external pipeline (`rfn_generator`, `rfn-video-pipeline`, `add_subtitles.py`) whose scripts are not in the repo, so what they check or do cannot be judged.

## Findings

### Needed
1. **No AI-generated or fictional-content disclosure before the video is made public** [model+map]
   Map node: comp.ai-disclosure - source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M14 Use AI responsibly"; comp.staged-content - source: 抖音规则中心 规则文件「【2023年3月】规则变更」, "虚假摆拍"
   Why: Searched for "AI label", "disclos", "altered", "fictional": nothing. The output is hyper-realistic generated actors in an everyday drama ("hyperrealistic", "realistic Korean facial features") that could be read as real.
2. **Originality and copyright handled as a fixed "30% change" rule** [model+map]
   Map node: assets.copyright-fair-use - source: YouTube for Creators: Helpful Resources, "Copyright"; comp.originality-reposts - source: bilibili社区公约 (创作中心), "认真创作"; short.original-content - source: YouTube for Creators: Shorts, "Ineligible Shorts views" (also: short.remix, comp.copyright-strike)
   Why: adaptation-guide says "저작권 침해 없이 ... 최소 30% 이상 변경이 필요". No percentage is a legal or platform test; platforms judge reused or near-identical content, and the method deliberately keeps the reference's arc, open-loop count, pacing and payoff timing. Nothing says to check the result against the reference or the platform's reused-content rule.
3. **Generated video clips are assembled and subtitled without being watched** [model+map+trace]
   Map node: edit.review-footage - source: O*NET 31.0 Database, task statements for video-related occupations, "27-4032.00 task: Review footage sequence by sequence to become familiar with it before assembling it into a final product."; short.quality-floor - source: 抖音社区自律公约, "视频画质模糊，无完整内容"; asm.review-revision - source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M10 review-and-revision workflow" (also: script.ai-assisted)
   Why: Step 5 requires visual checking of frames; Step 7 runs "run.py --resume" then "add_subtitles.py" and the output tree ends at "final_subtitled.mp4" with no review of clips or of the final file (blur, artefacts, off-story motion, subtitle position).
4. **Audio path is unconnected: TTS and MusicFX are named but no step feeds them into the pipeline output** [model+map+trace]
   Map node: sound.narration-recording - source: O*NET 31.0 Database, task statements for video-related occupations, "27-4011.00 task: Perform narration of productions or present announcements."; sound.music-selection - source: YouTube for Creators: Shorts, "Choose your music"; assets.music-licensing - source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M1 music-licensing and copyright basics" (also: short.music-eligibility, short.claims-over-one-minute)
   Why: Step 7 lists Google Cloud TTS, MusicFX and Google Vids as the stack, while the pipeline output tree contains only frames, clips, final.mp4 and final_subtitled.mp4. No step produces an audio file, mixes it, or says which editor assembles the final cut, so which path delivers sound is undecidable from the skill.
5. **Text-in-image fix exists only in the HTML storyboard, not in the video** [model+map+trace]
   Map node: captions.silent-viewing - source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M13 captions-on viewing"; short.safe-zone - source: TikTok Business Help Center: Creative best practices for performance ads (last updated June 2025), "keeping your content visible within the UI safe zone"; short.text-pace - source: TikTok Business Help Center: Creative best practices for performance ads (last updated June 2025), "5-10 words per second"
   Why: "AI 텍스트 한계 대응" adds CSS overlays for Korean text such as the fridge note, but those overlays are in the storyboard page; the generated clips and the burned-in subtitles have no equivalent, so the key payoff note in the story can still be garbled. No rule on subtitle placement or length either.
