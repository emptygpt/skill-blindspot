## Findings

### Needed
1. **No AI-content disclosure or labelling** [model+map]
   Map nodes: comp.ai-disclosure, comp.staged-content
   Why: The method generates narration with local TTS, reference voices, AI images and AI music, and often avoids visible AI labels ("不自动附加制作身份标签或创作自述"). It says to keep "必要事实边界和授权检查" but nothing tells the agent to label synthetic voice, synthetic people or AI-made scenes for the platform or viewer. Searched 披露, AI生成, 声明, AIGC across SKILL.md and references.

### Worth considering
1. **Title, cover, description and tags outside the interview route** [model+map]
   Map nodes: pack.thumbnail, pack.title, pack.title-type, pack.tags, pack.description-chapters
   Why: Only interview-commentary-direction.md has a cover section; no packaging for product promos or knowledge explainers, even though they are made for feeds ("信息流科普与宣传").
2. **Platform content rules and advertiser/sponsor checks for promos** [model+map]
   Map nodes: comp.community-guidelines, comp.submission-rules, comp.advertiser-friendly, comp.sponsor-disclosure, comp.violation-categories, comp.minors, comp.dangerous-content, comp.declare-original-repost
   Why: Review checks structure, semantic, visual, playback, audio and comprehension; none checks the platform's content rules or ad-claim wording for product promos.
3. **Audience research and a data feedback loop** [model+map]
   Map nodes: topic.audience-research, ana.insights
   Why: Audience is inferred from context; "comprehension: 真实反馈/实验；没做则not_run" is acknowledged but nothing gathers it; post-publish data is not in scope.
4. **Platform-specific cuts and copyright claim handling** [map]
   Map nodes: pub.one-to-many, comp.copyright-strike
   Why: Delivers one video at one ratio; no variants or claim handling.
