# Blindspot report: paper2video (V12)

Map: video-long 0.2.1 (extends video), combined with base map; Profile: none    Date: 2026-11-17

Audit target: SunandBean/paper2video, SKILL.md plus references/verification.md, licensing.md, scene-authoring.md. Scripts (p2v, scripts/*.py, engine/) were not run; only their names and descriptions in the skill were used.

## Scope
- Turns one arXiv paper into a narrated, subtitled, fact-checked explainer MP4 (1080p, 4-5 min default) with a verification report.
- Starts when the user gives a paper; ends at a rendered MP4 and a final report. "The skill never publishes anything."
- Does not film people, write titles/thumbnails for a platform, or track results. Voice is synthesised (Kokoro or Supertonic).

## Step 2 list (written before the map was opened)
Searched SKILL.md and the three references.
1. Opening hook and promise in the first seconds. Found only "Show where before how" and paper-order outline.
2. Audience definition and why-this-paper decision. User picks the paper; no audience question ("Do not ask for more instructions").
3. Audio QA of the final file: loudness, clipping, mispronunciation check of TTS. Found "Spoken numbers count too" only.
4. A separate caption file (SRT/VTT) for platforms. Subtitles are drawn into the picture.
5. AI-voice or synthetic-media disclosure note for the uploader.
6. Chapters or sections for the 4-5 min video.
7. Retention/pacing review of the rendered video.
8. Mobile legibility check of on-screen text (font size, contrast). Found only overflow/overlap checks and a clear subtitle band.
9. Title, thumbnail, description text. Only the description credit line in licensing.md.
10. Music/SFX. None used; licence rule exists.
11. Post-publication feedback. Out of scope.
12. End card or call to action. Not found.

15. Fonts for Korean/CJK rendering (found: "needs a Korean font", no licence note).

## Findings

### Needed
1. **The final MP4 audio is never checked** [model+map+trace]
   Map node: sound.mix, asm.av-sync, asm.platform-standards — source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M10 an audio mix"
   Why: Every check (check_claims, preview sheets, verifier layers) looks at text, numbers and pictures. Nothing measures loudness, clipping, silence gaps or A/V length on the rendered file, and nothing has a person or model listen for TTS mispronunciations beyond number spelling. Searched "loud", "LUFS", "audio check": no match.

### Worth considering
2. **No opening hook and no retention plan** [model+map]
   Map node: script.hook, script.audience-retention, long.open-close-key-moments — source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M1 hooks"
   Why: The outline "follows the paper's own logic"; Layer 3 checks order and fidelity only. Nothing asks for a first-seconds promise, a viewer takeaway, or where attention may drop in a 4-5 minute video.
3. **No caption file and no accessibility standard for on-screen text** [model+map]
   Map node: captions.create, captions.accuracy-accessibility, captions.silent-viewing, captions.translate — source: YouTube Help: Add subtitles & captions, "Create subtitles & captions"
   Why: Subtitles are drawn into frames. Platforms need a text track for search, translation and accessibility; none is exported. Layer 4 checks overflow, not contrast or phone-size legibility.
4. **No synthetic-media note and no audience statement** [model+map]
   Map node: comp.ai-disclosure, topic.focus, topic.project-definition — source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M14 Use AI responsibly"
   Why: The voice is synthetic and visuals are generated, yet the final report does not tell the uploader to set platform AI labels. No question of who the video is for ("Do not ask for more instructions").

### Minor
5. **Chapters and end card for a 4-5 minute video; music and SFX absent by design; font licence** [model+map]
   Map node: long.chapters, sound.music-selection, sound.effects, assets.font-licensing, edit.principles — source: YouTube Help: Video Chapters, "To add your own video chapters"
   Why: Not mentioned. Music and SFX are deliberately absent; list here so the author can exclude them.
6. **Remaining absent or shallow nodes, not individually argued** [map]
   Nodes: script.story-message, assets.generative-material, edit.rough-cut, edit.titles-transitions, captions.reach, pack.description-chapters, asm.timecode-check, pub.export, pub.archive, long.end-screen-timing
   Why: no matching instruction in the skill; each is either outside what an author of this skill is likely to add, or a small addition. Exclude in a profile if intentional.
