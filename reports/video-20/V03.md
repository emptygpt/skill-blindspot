The skill is large; "covered" below is judged on SKILL.md lines. Recipe READMEs may add detail not counted.

## Scope
- Turns one raw phone recording of a person talking to camera into a finished vertical 1080x1920 reel: best-take choice, pause trimming, word-timed captions off the face, motion scenes, SFX and memes, checked B-roll, preview, render, QA, delivery to a local folder.
- Responsibility ends at a rendered Instagram-spec master filed as "NOT POSTED"; it does not upload, schedule or read post performance.
- Does not film, does not write the speaker's words (they are the speaker's), not for landscape screen recordings.

## Findings

### Needed
1. **No label or disclosure step for AI-generated stills, voices and cut-outs** [model+map]
   Map node: comp.ai-disclosure, assets.generative-material — source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M14 Use AI responsibly"
   Why: Searched for disclosure, label, synthetic. The skill allows "AI stills", an ElevenLabs narrator recipe and AI cut-out effects but only limits what they may show. Platforms the description names (TikTok, Reels, Shorts) ask for labels on realistic altered media.

### Worth considering
1. **The speaker's own claims are never checked before the reel is built** [model+map]
   Map node: comp.fact-check, comp.media-law-ethics — source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M14 verifying AI-drafted facts"
   Why: "Nothing on screen says anything the speaker did not say" keeps the edit faithful, but nothing asks whether the speaker said something wrong, defamatory or sponsored. Only the Data Story recipe requires sources.
2. **No feedback from post performance** [model+map]
   Map node: ana.insights, ana.tracking — source: YouTube for Creators: Optimize your content, "Let insights guide your next move"
   Why: The "taste" loop learns from the user's corrections only; TRACKER records files moved, not views or retention.
3. **Posting text and sponsor disclosure** [model+map]
   Map node: pack.title, pack.title-type, pack.tags, comp.sponsor-disclosure, comp.honest-review, comp.offsite-leads — source: YouTube Help: Thumbnail & title tips, "How to write titles"
   Why: The skill demos named tools (Product page, stan style) and sets a URL end card with no disclosure or link-rule step; no post title or hashtags.

   Map node: pub.one-to-many, short.duration-limit, short.claims-over-one-minute, short.music-eligibility — source: Video Production Course Online (Self-Paced) syllabus, "M14 one-to-many platform cuts"
